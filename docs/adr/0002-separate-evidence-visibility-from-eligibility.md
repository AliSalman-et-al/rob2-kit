# Separate evidence visibility from eligibility

Evidence retrieval exposes every bounded candidate within mechanically authorized Source and Parse scope, including uncertain fragments, bibliography, and material classified as another Trial; parser and semantic labels may annotate or rank candidates but never hide them or authorize citation. Attributable candidate-and-exact-span review supplies Trial and Result semantics, while the engine alone enforces source-preserving canonicalization, provenance, bounds, custody, authorization, review completeness, immutable freeze, and dependency invalidation; this deliberately trades aggressive filtering and reconstruction for higher recall with fail-closed Evidence eligibility. Canonicalization, retrieval, review, and policy contracts are versioned, so historical runs remain reproducible under their original revisions and explicit reprocessing creates new dependent identities rather than reinterpreting prior evidence in place.
