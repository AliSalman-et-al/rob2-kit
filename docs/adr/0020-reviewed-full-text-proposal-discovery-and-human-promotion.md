# Use reviewed full-text observations and human promotion for Run proposal discovery

Issue #120 showed that proposal generation produced no Outcome targets or Result candidates unless `rob2.yaml` already declared them, leaving callers to invent complete Result, Randomization, and Arm identities after confirmation. We add a staged proposal-discovery workflow that preserves the boundary between what a report says and what researchers intend: `prepare_run` first produces a non-confirmable Project initialization snapshot for Source-role review; only after roles are resolved does an attributable agent review each usable accepted `primary_report`, `secondary_report`, `clinical_study_report`, or `regulatory_document` PDF through bounded canonical navigation and submit Reported endpoint, Reported randomization, and linked Reported arm candidates with immutable provenance and a Proposal discovery coverage receipt. Protocols, SAPs, registries, supplements, and other Sources may corroborate or rank candidates but do not originate them.

Engine-authored Proposal-discovery navigation snapshots are immutable internal workflow evidence events, not semantic review submissions. They durably bind the active Work token to issued searches, surfaced units, reads, and continuations so coverage can be verified after restart without trusting a caller-authored receipt.

Reported candidates are advisory source observations. Agent acceptance attests that an observation faithfully represents its cited full text; it never binds Run identity. A receipt and every current candidate are keyed to the exact Source artifact hash and Parse identity, so a pre-confirmation PDF or Parse change retires the old inventory from the successor while retaining immutable history. One proposal-token-bound human promotion batch declares Outcome targets and atomically promotes each Randomization with its complete Arm set, after which the engine issues stable identities and an attributable semantic mapping step proposes Trial-specific Result candidates. Comparison remains Result-specific rather than becoming a standalone discovery type: a Reported endpoint candidate may reference experimental and comparator Arm candidates only when the report identifies that ordered contrast. A complete accepted mapping binds the endpoint's exact canonical unit, page, and span; construct wording may differ only under an explicit typed human synonym rationale. Complete accepted mappings may materialize a ResultSpec revision on confirmation; partial analyses proceed through structured Result resolution. Discovery disposition, mapping state, identity completeness, promotion, and confirmation remain separate axes.

Discovery uses two bounded modes: declared targets receive target-guided passes that retain competing constructs, time points, populations, and analyses; projects without targets receive structure-first discovery of report-designated primary and secondary endpoints and reported intervention-effect analyses. `get_work_context` publishes the engine-issued policy revision, exact required pass list, token-scoped canonical search results, and bounded read continuations; a receipt can only claim the searches and canonical units that the same WorkToken observed. Engine-owned mandatory passes and continuations determine completion, not a scientific top-k cutoff. Every surfaced candidate receives an attributable disposition, repeated statements within one Source collapse only when exact provenance proves they describe the same analysis, and cross-Source observations remain distinct with corroborating, conflicting, or updated relationships. No candidates, limited discovery, failed discovery, and unfinished coverage remain distinct typed Proposal limitations. Unfinished required discovery blocks confirmation; a terminal limitation is material when declared target or Trial-design identity cannot be mapped, but a Run with no promoted targets may still be confirmed with an explicit limitation and recovery action.

The operations are deliberately staged: `submit_proposal_discovery_review` is bound to the immutable Project initialization snapshot and an engine-issued work token, records candidates and coverage without requiring a proposal token, and emits the first `RunProposal` only after all required discovery is complete. Structured `submit_run_proposal` then records human promotions and selections as an immutable successor, `submit_result_mapping_review` records cross-Trial mappings as another successor, and `confirm_run_definition` confirms the exact final proposal. Bound Source, Parse, discovery policy, disposition, promotion, mapping, or proposed-meaning changes invalidate affected work or proposal tokens. Pre-confirmation candidate identities may be regenerated; confirmed Randomization and Arm identities remain stable historical identities. We reject parser or regex auto-binding, treating source endpoints as discovered Outcome targets, allowing caller-authored opaque design IDs, scanning every incidental table variable, and reusing post-confirmation Evidence receipts as proposal coverage.

## Consequences

The expanded proposal payload is explicitly versioned, Result-source validation accepts the same eligible full-text roles as discovery, and typed Proposal limitations replace silent empty arrays. Checked-in synthetic acceptance fixtures cover targetless discovery, non-synonymous endpoint ambiguity, multiple reports and cutoffs, multi-arm and multi-Randomization designs, complete and partial Result extraction, terminal discovery conditions, stale submissions, and invalid cross-Randomization references; real CHAARTED material remains a private replay rather than a redistributable fixture.
