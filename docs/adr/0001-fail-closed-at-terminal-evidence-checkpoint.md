# Fail closed at the terminal evidence checkpoint

Before publishing a Result assessment, the engine re-derives the active signaling questions from the latest committed answers and bound Logic pack and verifies that each has qualifying frozen supporting or contradicting Evidence, a qualifying Visual transcription, or an engine-verified complete No-information basis. Domain submission performs the same check early as a correctable atomic validation, while terminal materialization remains the authoritative publication gate. An unsupported Result becomes diagnostic-ready without Domain or Overall judgments; historical revisions remain preserved and may be superseded after corrected dependencies are supplied. This deliberately favors traceable diagnostic incompleteness over publishing a deterministic judgment whose answer has no citable basis.
