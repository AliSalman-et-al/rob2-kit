Figure S1 describes a classification procedure at **36 weeks’ postmenstrual age**. Before testing, the baby must have received oxygen and/or respiratory support for **at least 28 days**. The test is not performed if the baby is receiving mechanical ventilation (invasive or noninvasive), CPAP, or high-flow oxygen; has FiO₂ ≥0.3 or low-flow oxygen ≥1.1 L/min; or needed respiratory support in the previous 24 hours. The legend and Table S4 likewise say only babies below those oxygen thresholds and without recent respiratory support are tested. [Appendix `sh_f5de7e58b7a415fc`, physical p. 8, Fig. S1; physical p. 14, Table S4]

The figure’s routes are:
- **Under 28 days’ oxygen/support:** “No bronchopulmonary dysplasia”; do not test; complete sections A, B, C and E.
- **At least 28 days, but breathing room air:** “Mild BPD”; do not test; complete A, B, C and E.
- **At least 28 days and on oxygen:** if requirements are outside the testable range, “Severe BPD”; do not test; complete A, B, C and E. If within the testable range, gradually reduce oxygen to the minimum level at which saturation can be maintained at **≥91% for at least 10 minutes**. If the baby is in air, classify as mild BPD; if still in **22–29% oxygen or 0.01–1.0 L/min**, classify as moderate BPD. The figure sends these tested routes to sections A–E. [Appendix `sh_f5de7e58b7a415fc`, physical p. 8, Fig. S1; physical p. 14, Table S4]

Table S4 defines the severity criteria at 36 weeks as therapy with oxygen >21% and/or respiratory support for ≥28 days, followed by those mild/moderate/severe categories. The main report identifies its primary outcome as **death or moderate/severe BPD** at 36 weeks; Fig. S1’s BPD classifications therefore distinguish the BPD component’s severity, while death is a separate component. [Appendix `sh_f5de7e58b7a415fc`, physical p. 14, Table S4; main report `sh_ec3fcba1a06e0ddc`, physical p. 3]
