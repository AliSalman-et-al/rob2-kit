# Scientific source audit (bounded sample)

Scope: representative D2–D5 disagreements in the 2026-09-27 Luna Medium benchmark, checked against source PDFs, final canonical traces, and the frozen provisional catalog. These are source-based audit observations, not expert-adjudicated truth. The RoB 2 assessment is result-specific. In D5, the core concern is selection among multiple eligible estimates based on direction, magnitude, or statistical significance; protocol/report mismatch alone does not prove selection ([Cochrane Handbook, chapter 8](https://training.cochrane.org/handbook/current/chapter-08)).

## General failure types and capacity hypotheses

- In this sample, D4 disagreement often comes from actual group differences in follow-up cadence, laboratory testing, or exposure duration. Distinguish ascertainment opportunity from bias direction and magnitude; do not automatically call these hallucinations.
- D5 calls are sensitive to missing result-plan chronology. The source packet often contains substantial protocol/SAP evidence, yet no dated record of when unblinded data became available. NI can be the correct answer to the signalling question; whether the resulting domain judgement matches the provisional catalog is a separate question.
- TITAN AE D3 is an informative trace-audit case: an NI answer about likely dependence is the explicit High driver. The source packet does not map all-data losses to AE-window completion. This warrants review of the conservative inference, but RoB 2's NI branches mean it is not enough to claim the model's High is formally wrong.
- The examples do not isolate Luna Medium capacity. The agent retrieved relevant facts and expressed source uncertainty; disagreements mostly involve interpreting RoB 2 thresholds and choosing severity when evidence is incomplete. A controlled same-evidence comparison at Medium vs High is needed before attributing these to reasoning capacity.

For the five audited outcome/trial pairs, the provisional expected labels stayed fixed. Comparing `all-case-accuracy.json` from Sept 26 with `accuracy.json` from Sept 27: TITAN AE D3 remained High; STAMPEDE AE D4 High and D5 Some Concerns remained unchanged; PEACE-1 AE D4 High remained unchanged while D5 moved Low→Some Concerns; STAMPEDE PFS D3 High, D4 Some Concerns→High, and D5 Some Concerns remained; PEACE-1 PFS D3 High and D5 Some Concerns remained, while D1 moved Low→Some Concerns. The changed D4/D5 labels are evidence that evidence framing or workflow can alter judgments between runs; they do not by themselves identify which run is more scientifically accurate. This small source-audited sample cannot support a causal capacity claim.
