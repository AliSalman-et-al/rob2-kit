# Offline factual source audit design

**Decision:** keep host-assisted finalization as a successful operational result and citation repair as an unmet objective. The successful supplied-evidence panel and native review were not equivalent tasks. A deterministic, opt-in, separate factual audit context is feasible for narrative-linked saved claims, but is an untested hypothesis; do not enable a default reviewer stage or claim attribution improvement.

No model calls, Baby-OSCAR assessment continuations, database edits or bundle changes occurred this turn. MCP remains model-free. The prototype only exports an input artifact. Feedback, if a future authorized experiment produces any, goes through ordinary review; it cannot automatically rewrite labels or citations.

## What actually differed

| History | New isolated session with supplied evidence; no previous assessment trajectory | Whole inherited two-turn assessment, including interrupted turn and saved judgments; current skill and generic completion prompt |

The native summary and D3 prefix carried a handle or text from the correct source, while the D4 prefix omitted the relevant warrant. Thus “production never had the repair page” is false, and “native review examined the same claim/source pair but failed” is also unsupported. The panel's alternative-source curation makes its success insufficient evidence for a citations-only checker recovering a correct replacement. Its explicit, narrower task and complete pairing are additional observable differences. We have no controlled evidence attributing the outcome to anchoring or model capacity.

This corrects the earlier continuation report's overly broad statement that the model inspected the relevant D4 source-support pair. The factual defect and operational finalization observations remain valid. `comparison.json`, `native-measurement-review.json` and the immutable native trace show the delivery boundary; `compare.py` recomputes it.

## Deterministic prototype and exact limitations

`python -m scripts.export_factual_audit BUNDLE TRIAL DOMAIN` verifies the immutable finalized bundle, exports **every saved answer** in that Domain, preserves its full warrant, unknowns, counterevidence and optional structured missing-data payload, and joins existing evidence identities to exact cited narrative spans. It records source identity/hash, physical page and line range, the captured text, domain/bundle identity and target/reported scope. Shared cited spans are emitted once; citation role assertions stay visible without being treated as entailment judgments. It does not include Domain/overall labels as audit targets.

The frozen D4 export includes three whole saved warrants, including the supported assessor-blinding claim and qualified method reasoning. It has five unique cited spans. It contains neither an operator-picked bad clause nor an alternative repair source. The correct numerical page is not a D4 citation and therefore is not inserted. An audit could correctly report that the cited passages do not establish the counts, but cannot establish their truth or a correct replacement from this input alone.

Canonical links are **answer-level**, not clause-level: the exporter cannot determine which clause a citation supports, prove entailment, or automatically find uncited support. Structured missing-data payloads are preserved, but their embedded provenance is not independently expanded unless linked by the answer's bases. Non-narrative evidence, missing source identity, missing linkage or inexact line numbering cause an explicit export error rather than a misleading complete text audit. Figure interpretation needs a separately designed visual protocol. This prototype consumes verified finalized bundles; it does not add a live partial-workspace API.

The saved full clauses still contain risk-related interpretation in the warrant; the instruction confines the task to source support and explicitly forbids reassignment of risk judgments. Claims such as “similar proportions” or a structured method's clinical relevance can be legitimate qualified inferences. A role called `direct_support` is not a factual guarantee. Nothing in this design turns missing support into a contradiction or a wrong clinical judgment.

## Cost and comparison

The deterministic D4 input is **29,317 UTF-8 bytes / 29,219 characters**, versus the original panel's 36,572 bytes across three selected items. Exact model token count is unavailable without inference or the launcher's tokenizer. Two sizing heuristics give approximately **7,305 tokens** (characters/4) or **13,473** (scaled by the observed panel's bytes/input-token ratio, including its host/instruction overhead); neither is an exact count or a defensible bound.

The panel used one response, zero tools, 16,807 input and 663 output tokens. The native continuation used one model turn, 13 tools, 765,107 cumulative input (683,520 cached; 81,587 uncached) and 2,476 output tokens. These are differently scoped calls. A future standalone audit would add **one response, zero tools**, roughly the input sizes above, plus output and host overhead. It would not remove the assessment cost or inherit its cached-history benefit. Export itself has zero inference cost. No quantitative scientific gain or cost saving is established.

## Proposed bounded experiment — requires authorization

1. Freeze this all-claims D4 export, exporter/source/bundle hashes, generic factual-audit instruction and criteria **before** any response. Do not append main p7, isolate the numeric sentence, mark a negative/control, or modify scope after seeing output. Declare the research question narrowly: can a fresh, citations-only, all-claims context identify unsupported numerical attribution while retaining supported facts and defensible inference?
2. Permit exactly one new isolated tool-free `gpt-6-luna` Medium response, at most 2,000 output tokens, 120 seconds wall, no retry/continuation/tools/search. Use the existing `launch_checked` protocol with a manifest declaring every exported cited span. Preflight proves captured coverage, not sufficiency for verifying all clauses. If additional uncited evidence is scientifically required for a stronger question, the citations-only input does not satisfy it; change the question before authorization rather than hand-curating a repair page.
3. Require per-question clause findings with exact cited locators and classifications: direct support, qualified support, legitimate inference, not established in supplied citations, explicit contradiction, or unresolved. All three warrants must receive findings. Distinguish lack of support from falsehood and plan from performance. Do not demand a numerical replacement or changed Domain label.
4. Freeze the response before offline adjudication. Private criteria score detection of the unsupported numerical attribution, preservation of directly supported assessor blinding, preservation of qualified method reasoning and the legitimate similarity inference, no fabricated locators, no false contradiction, and explicit incomplete-source limits. Exact replacement cannot be a success requirement because its support is absent from this export. Adjudicators may inspect preserved full sources, but their alternative pages stay outside model input.
5. Report coverage, true/false support findings, uncertainty, actual usage and violations independently from clinical judgment correctness. Compare with the panel and the **incomplete native delivery** as descriptive evidence only. This would not isolate task focus from history, obligation or evidence-selection effects. An incomplete response is an incomplete audit, not an implicit pass. No default adoption from one response.
6. Preserve any feedback as a separate artifact tied to original identities. A future ordinary review may decide whether to revise; this experiment neither reopens the finalized Baby-OSCAR assessment nor automatically repairs canonical data. No more assessment continuations are proposed.

The supported-source and inferential clauses in the entire chosen D4 Domain serve as controls without selecting only favorable sentences. A broader future plan-versus-conduct control should export a whole verified DAPA-HF D5 Domain, if available, rather than manually extracting the already-known control sentence. That additional experiment is not included or authorized here.
