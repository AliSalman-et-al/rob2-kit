8|I.
9|Death

11|A. Cardiovascular Death
12|
13|Cardiovascular death includes death classified in any of the following categories:
14|
15|1. Death due to Acute Myocardial Infarction refers to a death by any CV mechanism (e.g.,
16|arrhythmia, sudden death, HF, stroke, pulmonary embolus, peripheral arterial disease) ≤ 30 days
17|after a MI, related to the immediate consequences of the MI, such as progressive HF or recalcitrant
18|arrhythmia. We note that there may be assessable mechanisms of CV death during this time
19|period, but for simplicity, if the CV death occurs ≤ 30 days of the MI, it will be considered a death
20|due to MI.
21|Acute MI should be verified to the extent possible by the diagnostic criteria for acute MI or by
22|autopsy findings showing recent MI or recent coronary thrombosis.
23|Death resulting from a procedure to treat a MI (percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI), coronary
24|artery bypass graft surgery (CABG)), or to treat a complication resulting from MI, should also be
25|considered death due to acute MI.
26|Death resulting from an elective coronary procedure to treat myocardial ischemia (i.e., chronic
27|stable angina) or death due to a MI that occurs as a direct consequence of a CV
28|investigation/procedure/operation should be considered as a death due to a CV procedure.
29|
30|2. Sudden Cardiac Death refers to a death that occurs unexpectedly and not within 30 days of an
31|acute MI. Sudden cardiac death includes the following scenarios:
32|a. Death witnessed and occurring without new or worsening symptoms
33|
34|b. Death witnessed within 60 minutes of the onset of new or worsening cardiac symptoms, unless
35|the symptoms suggest acute MI
36|c. Death witnessed and attributed to an identified arrhythmia (e.g., captured on an
37|electrocardiographic (ECG) recording, witnessed on a monitor, or unwitnessed but found on
38|implantable cardioverter-defibrillator review)
39|
40|d. Death after unsuccessful resuscitation from cardiac arrest (e.g., implantable cardioverter
41|defibrillator (ICD) unresponsive sudden cardiac death, pulseless electrical activity arrest)
42|e. Death after successful resuscitation from cardiac arrest and without identification of a specific
43|cardiac or non-cardiac etiology
44|f. Unwitnessed death in a subject seen alive and clinically stable ≤ 24 hours prior to being found
