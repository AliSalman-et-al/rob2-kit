8|Assessment of Proportional Hazards Assumptions
9|
10|There was a statistically significant violation of the proportional hazards assumption (p=0.006). Visually, the
11|survival curves separate early and remain relatively parallel throughout the duration of the study without
12|notable convergence. Similarly, visual inspection of the "log-log" survival plot, i.e. the -log(-log(survival)) vs
13|log(time) showed similar, relatively minor deviations from the assumption of parallel lines. Finally, a robust
14|model-free alternative comparison based on a comparison of the areas under the KM curves up to 3 years of
15|follow-up showed a relative treatment effect estimate (Ratio = 0.80, 95% CI: 0.71-0.89)."
