18|III.
19|Urgent Heart Failure Visit is defined as an event that meets all of the following:
20|
21|1) The patient has an urgent, unscheduled office/practice or emergency department visit for a primary
22|diagnosis of HF, but not meeting the criteria for a HF hospitalization
23|2) The patient meets all signs and symptoms for HF hospitalization [i.e., 3) symptoms,
24|4) physical examination findings/laboratory evidence of new or worsening HF, as indicated
25|above]
26|3) The patient receives at least ONE of the following treatments specifically for HF:
27|a. Initiation of intravenous diuretic or vasoactive agent (e.g., inotrope, vasopressor, or vasodilator)*
28|b. Mechanical or surgical intervention, including:
29|i.
30|Mechanical circulatory support (e.g., intra-aortic balloon pump, ventricular assist
31|device, extracorporeal membrane oxygenation, total artificial heart)
32|ii.
33|Mechanical fluid removal (e.g., ultrafiltration, hemofiltration, dialysis)
34|
35|*Note that significant augmentation of oral diuretic therapy will NOT be sufficient to fulfill the urgent HF visit
36|criteria.

38|General Considerations (Urgent HF Visit)
39|
40|1. Clinic visits for scheduled administration of HF therapies or procedures (e.g., intravenous diuretics,
41|intravenous vasoactive agents, or mechanical fluid removal) do NOT qualify as non- hospitalized HF events.
