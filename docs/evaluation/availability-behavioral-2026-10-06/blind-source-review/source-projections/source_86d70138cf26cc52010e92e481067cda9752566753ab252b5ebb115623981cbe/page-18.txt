23|C. Undetermined Death
24|
25|This refers to any death not attributable to one of the above categories of cardiovascular death or to a
26|non-cardiovascular cause (e.g., due to lack of information such as a case where the only information
27|available is "patient died"). It is expected that every effort will be made to provide the adjudicating
28|committee with enough information to attribute deaths to either a cardiovascular or non-
29|cardiovascular cause so that the use of this category is kept to a minimal number of patients.

32|Heart Failure Events
33|
34|Heart Failure Hospitalization is defined as an event that meets ALL of the following criteria:
35|1) The patient is admitted to the hospital with a primary diagnosis of HF (in the opinion of the CEA)
36|
37|2) The patient's length of stay in hospital extends for at least 24 hours (or a change in calendar
38|date if the hospital admission and discharge times are unavailable)
39|
40|3) The patient exhibits documented new or worsening symptoms due to HF on presentation, including at
41|least ONE of the following:
42|a. Dyspnea (dyspnea with exertion, dyspnea at rest, orthopnea, paroxysmal nocturnal dyspnea)
