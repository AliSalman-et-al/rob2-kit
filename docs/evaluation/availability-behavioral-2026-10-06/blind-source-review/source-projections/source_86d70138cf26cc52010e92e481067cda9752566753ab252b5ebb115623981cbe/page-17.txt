9|General Considerations
10|
11|- Unless additional information suggests an alternate specific cause of death (i.e. Death due
12|to Other Cardiovascular Causes), if a patient is seen alive ≤ 24 hours of being found dead, sudden
13|cardiac death (criterion 2f) should be recorded. For patients who were not observed alive within 24
14|hours of death, undetermined cause of death should be recorded (e.g., a subject found dead in
15|bed, but who had not been seen by family for > 24 hours).
16|3. Death due to Heart Failure refers to a death in association with clinically worsening symptoms
17|and/or signs of HF regardless of HF etiology. Deaths due to HF can have various etiologies, including
18|single or recurrent MIs, ischemic or non-ischemic cardiomyopathy, hypertension, or valvular
19|disease.
20|4. Death due to Stroke refers to death after a stroke that is either a direct consequence of the stroke
21|or a complication of the stroke. Acute stroke should be verified to the extent possible by the
22|diagnostic criteria outlined for stroke.
23|5. Death due to Cardiovascular Procedures refers to death caused by the immediate complications
24|of a cardiac procedure.
25|6. Death due to Cardiovascular Hemorrhage refers to death related to hemorrhage such as a non-
26|stroke intracranial hemorrhage (e.g., subdural hematoma), non-procedural
27|or non-traumatic vascular rupture (e.g., aortic aneurysm), or hemorrhage causing cardiac
28|tamponade.
29|7. Death due to Other Cardiovascular Causes refers to a CV death not included in the above
30|categories but with a specific, known cause (e.g., pulmonary embolism or peripheral arterial
31|disease).
32|B. Non-Cardiovascular Death
33|
34|Non-cardiovascular death is defined as any death with a specific cause that is not thought to be CV in
35|nature. Non-cardiovascular deaths will be further classified into the following categories:
36|• Pulmonary
37|• Renal death due to ESRD where dialysis treatment was deliberately withheld (dialysis was not
38|started or discontinued) for any reason. E.g., patient refuses dialysis, treating physician considers
39|the dialysis futile, or dialysis is not available. If death is related to other causes than ESRD, the death
40|will NOT be adjudicated as renal cause of death.
41|• Gastrointestinal - Death due to GI-related abnormalities unrelated to liver or pancreas
