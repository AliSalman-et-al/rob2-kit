20|Safety Data Collection
21|Serious adverse events (SAE) and adverse events leading to discontinuation of study drug (DAE) were collected. In
22|addition, prespecified AEs were events of volume depletion, renal events, diabetes ketoacidosis, major hypoglycemia
23|and lower limb amputations. The Data source for the safety events listed in table 2 of the manuscript are as follows:
24|Adjudicated: Diabetic ketoacidosis (definite or probable)
25|Case report forms were used to analyze major hypoglycemia and events leading to lower limbs amputations .
26|Pre-defined preferred term lists for DAE (AEs leading to treatment discontinuation) /SAE of volume depletion
27|events and renal events
28|Major hypoglycemia was defined as an event where all the following criteria were confirmed by the investigator:
29|(1) the patient experienced symptoms of severe impairment in consciousness or behavior; (2) the patient
30|needed external assistance; (3) intervention was needed to treat the hypoglycemia; and (4) there was prompt
31|recovery of acute symptoms following the intervention.
32|The independent, blinded diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA) adjudication committee consisting of content experts in the
33|medical area of interest, adjudicated all events of potential diabetic ketoacidosis and reported events are those
34|adjudicated to be definite or probable DKA.
35|A separate DKA Adjudication Manual defined and described the procedures for the collection of DKA information,
36|handling, adjudication criteria and reporting of these events.
