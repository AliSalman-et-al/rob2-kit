19|Efficacy event adjudication
20|A Clinical Events Adjudication HCEAI Committee will be established for this trial and adjudicate
21|primary efficacy events in accordance with adjudication criteria detailed in the CEA charter.

23|hospitalisation for HF, and urgent HF visits. All deaths will be adjudicated to determine if they are
24|CV or non-CV deaths. All adjudication will be done on an ongoing basis throughout the trial.
