17|C 2
18|Diagnosis of MI and Unstable Angina
19|Myocardial infarction (MI)
20|The diagnosis of an MI should be made according to standard clinical practice but is expected to
21|align with the criteria from Third Universal Definition of MI, i.e. detection of a rise and/or fall of
22|cardiac biomarkers such as troponin and at least one of the following: typical clinical symptoms,
23|ischaemic ECG findings, imaging evidence of myocardial injury, or detection of an intracoronary
24|thrombus by angiography or autopsy (Thygesen et al 2012).
25|The diagnosis should be made by, or in consultation with, a cardiologist. The findings supporting
26|the diagnosis should be documented in the description of the SAE in the eCRF.
