13|INTERIM ANALYSES
14|An interim analysis is planned to be performed including approximately 67I of the target
15|number of patients with adjudicated primary endpoint events. There will in principle be one
16|planned interim analysis for efficacyH with the possibility of the data monitoring committee
17|(DMC) to conduct subseCuent interim analysis if they deem necessary. The significance level
18|for final analysis will be determined by the Haybittle-Peto function based on the actual
19|number of interim analysesH using the East software (Copyright U Cytel Inc). The interim
20|analysis will assess superiority of dapaglifloDin to placebo. The interim analysis will have a
21|nominal two-sided alpha level of 0.2I. At the interim analysisH the primary composite
22|endpoint will be tested first at the specified alpha level. If superiority is achieved for the
23|primary endpointH then the superiority of dapaglifloDin to placebo on CV deaths will be tested
24|at a two-sided level of 0.2I. If CV death is significantH then an action is triggered whereby the
25|DMC will evaluate the totality of the efficacy data and safety dataH to determine if benefit is
26|uneCuivocal and overwhelming such that the DMC recommends ending the study.
27|If the interim analysis leads to a decision to terminate the study early based on pre-defined
28|stopping guidelinesH the interim analysis database will become the basis of statistical inference
29|for the primary endpoint and CV death. Following such a decisionH the executive committee
30|will define a PACDH on or after which study closure visits will commence. Analysis based on
31|the final database will be conducted to support the full reporting of the study. The consistency
32|between the interim analysis database and the subseCuently locked database will be assessed.
33|If the study is stopped in the efficacy interim analysisH testing of secondary endpoints will be
34|performed on the final database with the same testing procedure as described in section 4.1.3
35|with two-sided significance level 0.002.
36|A futility analysis is planned to be performed at the same time as the planned interim analysis.
37|The study may be stopped for futility if the observed HR for the primary endpoint is F 0.946H
38|corresponding to a predictive power of 5I. If the futility criterion of the primary endpoint is
39|metH then DMC will evaluate the totality of dataH including potential benefits on patient
40|reported outcomes to consider recommending ending the study for futility.
