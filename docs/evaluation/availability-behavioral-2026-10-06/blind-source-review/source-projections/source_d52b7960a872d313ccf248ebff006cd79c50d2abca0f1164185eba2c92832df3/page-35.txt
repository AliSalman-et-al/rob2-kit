11|7.1
12|Discontinuation of study treatment
13|Discontinuation from study treatment is NOT the same thing as a withdrawal from the study. If
14|the patient temporarily or permanently discontinues IP, the patient should remain in the study and
15|it is important that the scheduled study visits and data collection continue according to the study
16|protocol until study closure.
17|x Patients may be discontinued from IP in the following situations:
18|x Contraindication to further dosing with IP, in the opinion of the Investigator, such as
19|Adverse event or other safety reasons.
20|x Severe non-compliance with the study protocol.
21|x Diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA). Consider temporarily interrupting IP if DKA is suspected.
22|If DKA is confirmed, IP should be discontinued permanently.
23|x Positive pregnancy test (discontinue IP and notify Sponsor representative).
24|x Patient decision. The patient is at any time free to discontinue treatment, without prejudice
25|to further treatment.
26|See the Table 1 for data to be collected at the time of treatment discontinuation and follow-up and
27|for any further evaluations that need to be completed.

33|7.1.1.1
34|Unexpected acute declines in eGFR
35|If an unexpected, acute decline in kidney function is observed, the patient should be evaluated and
36|temporary interruption of IP should be considered. Volume depletion, hypotension, inter-current
37|medical problems and concomitant drugs may cause increases in blood creatinine. Urinary tract
38|infection and urinary obstruction should be considered (the latter especially in men). Several drugs
39|may cause a decline in kidney function, especially non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAID)
40|and certain antibiotics such as trimethoprim. If any drug is suspected of causing or contributing
41|to worsening kidney function, their use should be re-considered.
