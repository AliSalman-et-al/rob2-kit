6|Dapagliflozin reduces blood volume and blood pressure from its diuretic effect, which could be a
7|concern in patients with HFpEF, but also be important mechanisms of a potential treatment effect.
8|However, in the dapagliflozin type 2 diabetes mellitus HT2DI program, the rate of events related to
9|volume depletion and impaired renal function have been similar between dapagliflozin and placebo.
10|Loop-diuretics are widely used in the target patient population and are also allowed in this study. A
11|pooled analysis of patients with T2D and HF in the dapagliflozin development program, showed no
12|increase of volume depletion events but increase in renal events, mainly creatinine increases, in patients
13|treated with dapagliflozin HnX171I compared with placebo treated patients HnX149I. About half of the
14|patients were on loop diuretics HCosiborod et al 2017bI.
15|An increase in amputations, mostly affecting toes, was observed in a clinical trial HNeal et al 2017I
16|with another SGLT2 inhibitor. There is no indication from the clinical development program that
17|dapagliflozin is associated with an increased risk of amputation Hsee Section 8.5.1.1 for the
18|detection and capture of amputation eventsI.
19|Dapagliflozin has not been shown to induce hypoglycaemia in non-diabetes patients. In clinical
20|pharmacology studies, healthy subjects have been treated with single oral doses up to 500 mg and
21|multiple oral doses of 100 mg up to 14 days without any hypoglycaemic events.
22|There have been post-marketing reports of ketoacidosis, including diabetic ketoacidosis, in patients
23|with T2D taking dapagliflozin and other SGLT2 inhibitors, although a causal relationship has not
24|been established.
25|Patients treated with dapagliflozin who present with signs and symptoms consistent with
26|ketoacidosis, including nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, malaise, and shortness of breath, should
27|be assessed for ketoacidosis, even if blood glucose levels are below 14 mmolPL H250 mgPdLI. If
28|ketoacidosis is suspected interruption of dapagliflozin treatment should be considered and the
29|patient should be promptly evaluated.
30|Predisposing factors to ketoacidosis include a low beta-cell function reserve resulting from
31|pancreatic disorders He.g., T1D, history of pancreatitis, or pancreatic surgeryI, insulin dose
32|reduction, reduced caloric intake, or increased insulin requirements due to infections, illness or
33|surgery and alcohol abuse. Dapagliflozin should be used with caution in patients in these
34|circumstances. Dapagliflozin is currently not indicated for the treatment of patients with T1DR these
35|patients are excluded from this study.

39|potential risks to participating patients. Data regarding amputations and adverse events
40|potentially placing the patient at risk for a lower limb amputation will be collected Hsee Section

44|responsible for safeguarding the interests of the patients by reviewing safety data throughout the
45|study Hsee Section 9.5.1I.
