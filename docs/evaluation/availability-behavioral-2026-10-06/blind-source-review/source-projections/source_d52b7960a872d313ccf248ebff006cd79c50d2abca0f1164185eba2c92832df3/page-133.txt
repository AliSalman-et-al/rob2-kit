30|Procedures for discontinuation of study treatment
31|Investigators should instruct their patients to contact the site before or at the time IP is stopped. A
32|patient that decides to discontinue IP will always be asked about the reasonHsI and the presence of
33|any AEs. Generally, AEs, SAEs, and potential endpoint events should not lead to IP
34|discontinuation, unless there is a clear clinical rationale to do so.
35|The date of last intake of IP should be documented in the eCRF. All IP should be returned by the
36|patient at their next on-site study visit or unscheduled visit. Patients permanently discontinuing IP
37|should be given locally available standard of care therapy, at the discretion of the Investigator.
38|Discontinuation of IP, for any reason, does not impact on the patient[s participation in the study.
39|The patient should continue attending subsequent study visits and data collection should continue
40|according to the study protocol. If the patient does not agree to continue in-person study visits, a
41|modified follow-up must be arranged to ensure the collection of endpoints and safety information.
42|This could be a telephone contact with the patient, a contact with a relative or treating physician,
43|or information from medical records. The approach taken should be recorded in the medical
44|records. A patient that agrees to modified follow-up is not considered to have withdrawn from the
45|study.
