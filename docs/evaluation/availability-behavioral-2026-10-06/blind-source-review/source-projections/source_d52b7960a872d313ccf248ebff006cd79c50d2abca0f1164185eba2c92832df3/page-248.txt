8|PRIMARY AND SECONDARY VARIABLES
9|Deaths and potential HF events will be adjudicated by an independent CEA committee. The
10|CEA committee members will not have access to the treatment codes for any patient. The
11|CEA procedures and event definitions will be described in the CEA charter according to the
12|CDISC definitions (HicGs et al 2018).
13|Only HF hospitalisations and urgent HF visits confirmed by the CEA will be used in the
14|analysis of the primary and secondary endpoints and their components.
15|The primary analyses of the endpoints concerning CV deathsO either as a component of a
16|composite or on its ownO will include deaths adjudicated as CV cause. Deaths adjudicated as
17|Ecause undeterminedF will be considered as non-CV deaths in these analyses.
18|Adjudicated events occurring from randomisation until WoC or PACD will be included in the
19|analysis of primary and secondary endpoints. The analysis of all-cause death as a sole
20|outcome will in addition include any deaths (not adjudicated) after WoCO but on or before
21|PACD.
