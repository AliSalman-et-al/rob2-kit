20|Informed consent
21|The genetic component of this study is optional and the subject may participate in other
22|components of the main study without participating in the genetic component. To participate in the
23|genetic component of the study the subject must sign and date both the consent form for the main
24|study and the genetic component of the study. Copies of both signed and dated consent forms must
25|be given to the subject and the original filed at the study centre. The Principal InvestigatorHsI is
26|responsible for ensuring that consent is given freely and that the subject understands that they may
27|freely withdrawal from the genetic aspect of the study at any time.
28|Subject data protection
29|AstraZeneca will not provide individual genotype results to subjects, any insurance company, any
30|employer, their family members, general physician unless required to do so by law.
31|Extra precautions are taken to preserve confidentiality and prevent genetic data being linked to the
32|identity of the subject. In exceptional circumstances, however, certain individuals might see both
33|the genetic data and the personal identifiers of a subject. For example, in the case of a medical
34|emergency, an AstraZeneca Physician or an investigator might know a subjectOs identity and also
35|have access to his or her genetic data. In addition, Regulatory authorities may require access to the
36|relevant files, though the subjectOs medical information and the genetic files would remain
37|physically separate.
