8|Appendix B Adverse event definitions and additional safety information
9|B 1
10|Definition of adverse events
11|An adverse event is the development of any untoward medical occurrence in a subject or clinical
12|study subject administered a medicinal product and which does not necessarily have a causal
13|relationship with this treatment. An AE can therefore be any unfavourable and unintended sign
14|(e.g. an abnormal laboratory finding), symptom (for example nausea, chest pain), or disease
15|temporally associated with the use of a medicinal product, whether or not considered related to the
16|medicinal product.
17|The term AE is used to include both serious and non-serious AEs and can include a deterioration
18|of a pre-existing medical occurrence. An AE may occur at any time, including run-in or washout
19|periods, even if no Study treatment has been administered.

43|B 4
44|Hospitalisation
45|Outpatient treatment in an emergency room is not in itself a serious AE, although the reasons for
46|it may be (e.g., bronchospasm, laryngeal oedema). Hospital admissions and/or surgical operations
47|planned before or during a study are not considered AEs if the illness or disease existed before the
48|subject was enrolled in the study, provided that it did not deteriorate in an unexpected way during
49|the study.
