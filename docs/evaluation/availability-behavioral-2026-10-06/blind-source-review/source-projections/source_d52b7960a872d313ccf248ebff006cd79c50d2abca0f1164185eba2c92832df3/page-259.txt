14|Missing data and informative censoring
15|The time-to-event analysis using the Cox regression depends on the assumption of non-
16|informative or ignorable censoringO corresponding to the missing-at-random assumption. The
17|missing data in this context are patients who are prematurely censored due to WoCO LTFU or
18|otherwise incomplete follow-up of endpoints. The amount of missing data will be described
19|egO in terms of the number of patients and patient time with incomplete follow-up as described
20|in Section 4.1.5.
21|Patient retention and follow-up are at the forefront of study planning and conductO and the
22|amount of incomplete follow-up is expected to be small.
23|To assess the effect of incomplete follow up of the primary endpointO a sensitivity analysis
24|may be performed where time to event information is imputed for patients with premature
25|censoring (censored before PACD due to WoC or incomplete primary event assessment).
26|Event rates will be estimated separately in the two T2DM strata by an exponential distribution
27|with constant haIard rate over time. Using the haIard ratio from the primary analysisO the
28|event rates will be calculated for the dapaglifloIin groupO separately for the T2DM strata (by
29|multiplying the corresponding placebo group rates by the haIard ratio estimated in the primary
30|analysis). Using the estimated event ratesO new event times will be simulated for patients with
31|premature censoring from the exponential distribution. If the simulated time is in the interval
32|from the censoring date to PACD (or death dateO whichever came first)O a new event will be
33|imputed at the resulting event time. OtherwiseO if the simulated time is outside the interval
34|from the original censoring to PACD or deathO the patient will be considered censored at
35|PACD or death. The primary analysis will thereafter be conducted againO supplemented by the
36|simulated time-to-event information. The process is to be repeated 1000 times and the
37|resulting haIard ratios and standard errors will be combined using the RubinKs rule.
