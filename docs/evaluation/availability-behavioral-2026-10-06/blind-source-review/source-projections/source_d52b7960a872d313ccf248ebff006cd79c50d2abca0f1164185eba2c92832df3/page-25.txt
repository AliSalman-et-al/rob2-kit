10|Most randomised patients will be out-patients. However, to address a specific need in a period
11|with high risk for events, a proportion of patients will be enrolled and randomised during
12|hospitalisation for heart failure or within 21 days of discharge from hospitalisation for heart failure
13|(subacute subgroup).
14|The study population will include patients both with and without T2D, as the beneficial
15|haemodynamic effects of dapagliflozin appear to be independent of the glycaemic effect, and can
16|therefore be expected in both groups. Enrolment in the study may be capped based on the
17|proportion of patients with/without T2D, in certain LVEF categories, in each NYHA class,
18|with/without atrial fibrillation, randomised during or early after HF hospitalisation (subacute
19|subgroup), and geographic region.
20|The control group will receive placebo; there are no approved pharmacological treatments for
21|HFpEF that could be utilised as a comparator. All patients will be treated according to local
22|guidelines on standard of care treatment for patients with HFpEF, focusing on treatment of HF
23|symptoms (e.g. diuretics) and comorbidities (including treatment for high blood pressure,
24|ischaemic heart disease, atrial fibrillation).
