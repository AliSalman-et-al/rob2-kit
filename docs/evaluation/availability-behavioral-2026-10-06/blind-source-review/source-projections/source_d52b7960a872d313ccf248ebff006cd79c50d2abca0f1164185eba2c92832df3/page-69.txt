41|Medication Error
42|For the purposes of this clinical study a medication error is an unintended failure or mistake in the
43|treatment process for an AstraZeneca investigational product that either causes harm to the
44|participant or has the potential to cause harm to the participant.
45|A medication error is not lack of efficacy of the drug, but rather a human or process related failure
46|while the drug is in control of the study site staff or participant.
