21|Collection of samples for genetic research
22|The blood sample for genetic research will be obtained from the subjects at Visit 2. Although
23|DNA is stable, early sample collection is preferred to avoid introducing bias through excluding
24|subjects who may withdraw due to an adverse event (AE), such subjects would be important to
25|include in any genetic analysis. If for any reason the sample is not drawn at Visit 2, it may be
26|taken at any visit until the last study visit. Only one sample should be collected per subject for
27|genetics during the study. Samples will be collected, labelled, stored, and shipped as detailed in
28|the Laboratory Manual.
29|Coding and storage of DNA samples
30|The processes adopted for the coding and storage of samples for genetic analysis are important to
31|maintain subject confidentiality. Samples will be stored for a maximum of 15 years, from the date
32|of last subject last visit, after which they will be destroyed. DNA is a finite resource that is used
33|up during analyses. Samples will be stored and used until no further analyses are possible or the
34|maximum storage time has been reached.
35|An additional second code will be assigned to the blood sample either before or at the time of DNA
36|extraction replacing the information on the sample tube. Thereafter, the sample will be identifiable
37|only by the second, unique number. This number is used to identify the sample and corresponding
38|data at the AstraZeneca genetics laboratories, or at the designated organisation. No personal details
39|identifying the individual will be available to any person (AstraZeneca employee or designated
40|organisations working with the DNA).
41|The link between the subject enrolment/randomisation code and the second number will be
42|maintained and stored in a secure environment, with restricted access at AstraZeneca or designated
43|organisations. The link will be used to identify the relevant DNA samples for analysis, facilitate
44|correlation of genotypic results with clinical data, allow regulatory audit, and permit tracing of
45|samples for destruction in the case of withdrawal of consent.
