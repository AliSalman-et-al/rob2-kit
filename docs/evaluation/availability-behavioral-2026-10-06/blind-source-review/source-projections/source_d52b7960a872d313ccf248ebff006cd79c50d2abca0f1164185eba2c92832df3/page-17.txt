8|Treatments and treatment duration:
9|Patients will be randomised in a 1:1 ratio to receive either dapagliflozin 10 mg or placebo once
10|daily. The anticipated average treatment duration is 24 months (range 15 to 33 months).
11|Data Monitoring Committee:
12|An independent Data Monitoring Committee (DMC) will review accumulating trial data by
13|treatment group in order to monitor patient safety and efficacy, ensure the validity and integrity of
14|the trial, and make benefit-risk assessment.
15|Statistical methods
16|This study is event-driven. The primary objective of the study is to determine the superiority of
17|dapagliflozin versus placebo, when added to standard of care, in reducing the composite of CV
18|death and HF events (hospitalisation for HF or urgent HF visit). Assuming a true hazard ratio (HR)
19|of 0.80 between dapagliflozin and placebo, using a two-sided alpha of 5%, 844 primary endpoint
20|events will provide a statistical power of 90% for the test of the primary composite endpoint.
21|Approximately 4700 patients are estimated to provide the required number of primary events
22|during an anticipated recruitment period of 18 months and a minimal follow-up period of 15
23|months (total study duration 33 months, average follow-up 24 months). Randomisation will be
24|stratified by presence or absence of Type 2 Diabetes (T2D).
25|All patients who have been randomised to study treatment will be included in the Full Analysis
26|Set (FAS) irrespective of their protocol adherence and continued participation in the study. The
27|primary variable is the time to first event included in the primary composite endpoint. The primary
28|analysis will be based on the intention to treat (ITT) principle using the FAS, including events
29|occurring on or prior to the primary analysis censoring date (PACD), confirmed by adjudication.
30|In the analysis of the primary composite endpoint, treatments (dapagliflozin versus placebo) will
31|be compared using a Cox proportional hazards model with a factor for treatment group, stratified
32|by type 2 diabetes (T2D) status at randomisation. The p-value, hazard ratio and 95% confidence
33|interval will be reported.
34|Interim analysis of superiority and futility is planned to be performed including approximately
35|67% of target number of adjudicated primary endpoints.
36|A closed testing procedure including a pre-specified hierarchical ordering of the primary and
37|secondary endpoints will be utilized. No multiplicity control is placed on the exploratory
38|endpoints.
