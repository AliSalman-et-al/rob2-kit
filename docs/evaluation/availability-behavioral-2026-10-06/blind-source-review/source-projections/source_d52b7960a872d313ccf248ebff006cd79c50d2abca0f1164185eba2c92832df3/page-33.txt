8|6.3.1.1
9|Stratification
10|Randomisation will be stratified in IxRS based on patients with and without T2D at the time of
11|randomisation in order to ensure approximate balance between treatment groups within each
12|sub-population. Stratification on T2D at the time of randomisation is based on:
13|x Established diagnosis of T2D
14|OR
15|x HbA1c 6.5% (48 mmol/mol) VhoZn aW cenWUal laboratory test at enrolment (Visit 1)
16|6.3.1.2
17|Capping
18|The intent is to enrol a typical cross-section of patients with HFpEF and to include representative
19|proportions of diabetic and non-diabetic patients. The number of randomised patients with and
20|without T2D will be monitored in order to ensure a minimum of 30% in each sub-population.
21|Randomisation may be capped (i.e., no more patients can be randomised in a specific
22|sub-population) if the pre-determined limit is reached.
23|Randomisation of patients based on geographic region will be monitored to ensure global
24|representation. LVEF value, NYHA class, subacute subgroup (i.e. randomised in-hospital or
25|within 21 days from discharge) and atrial fibrillation status at Visit 1 may be capped in IxRS to
26|avoid over- or under-representation of these patient subgroups.
27|6.4
28|Treatment compliance
29|The administration of all IP should be recorded in the appropriate sections of the eCRF. Any
30|change from the dosing schedule should be recorded in the eCRF.
31|Patients will be asked to return all unused IP and empty packages to the clinic at the site visit
32|except Visit 3. At each visit, any patient found to be non-compliant will be counselled on the
33|importance of taking their IP as prescribed. The investigator or delegate will enter the number of
34|returned tablets in the eCRF.
35|The Investigational Product Storage Manager is responsible for managing IP from receipt by the
36|study site until the destruction or return of all unused IP. The Investigator(s) is responsible for
37|ensuring that the patient has returned all unused IP.
38|6.5
39|Concomitant therapy
40|All patients should be treated according to regional standard of care of HFpEF and existing
41|comorbidities (including treatment of hypertension, ischemic heart disease, atrial fibrillation,
42|diabetes, hyperlipidaemia). Background medications should be part of clinical practice and will
43|not be provided by the Sponsor.
