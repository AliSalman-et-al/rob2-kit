9|3.2.3
10|Proportion of patients with worsened NYHA class at 8 months
11|The efficacy variable is the proportion of patients with worsened NYHA class from baseline
12|to 8 months.
13|The NYHA classification will be evaluated by the investigator and collected in eCRF at
14|enrolment and randomisation visitsH at 1H 4 and 8 months visitsH at PTDV and SCV. Baseline
15|is defined as the value at randomisation (visit 2). The analysis will include all data irrespective
16|of whether the patient has discontinued study drug.
17|For the primary analysis the data will be dichotomised into patients with worsened NYHA
18|class at 8 months (the NYHA class is higher than baseline)H including patients who died due to
19|any cause prior to 8 monthsH versus other patients with improved or unchanged class
20|compared to baseline.
21|3.2.4
22|Death from any cause
23|The efficacy variable is time to from randomisation to death from any cause. All deaths on or
24|prior to PACDH including any deaths after WoCH will be included. Patients who are alive will
25|be censored at the earliest of date last known alive and PACD.
26|3.3
27|Safety variables
28|The safety and tolerability of dapaglifloDin in patients with HFpEF will be evaluated from
29|serious adverse events (SAEs)H adverse events leading discontinuation of IP (DAEs)H adverse
30|events(AE) leading to amputation and AEs reflecting potential risk factors for lower limb
31|amputations (Rpreceding eventsS).
32|In addition to amputationH non-serious and serious events potentially placing the patient at risk
33|for a lower limb amputationH in this document denoted Rpreceding eventsSH should also be
34|recorded in the eCRF as AE/SAEH whether or not an amputation has taken place. Preceding
35|events will be defined for analysis by a predefined list of preferred terms. Additional
36|information about amputations with underlying conditions and preceding events will be
37|collected on dedicated eCRF pages.
38|SAEs will be collected from time of informed consent until and including the patentOs last
39|visit. Non-serious AEs will be collected from randomisation until and including the patientOs
40|last visit. Collection of non-serious AEs is limited to AE leading to amputationH preceding
41|eventsH AEs leading to a potential endpointH DAEs and AEs which are the reason for
42|interruption of study drug.
