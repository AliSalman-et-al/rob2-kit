14|7.3
15|Withdrawal from the study
16|Patients are free to withdraw from the study at any time (IP and assessments), without prejudice
17|to further treatment. Withdrawal of consent should only occur if the patient has received
18|appropriate information about and does not agree to any kind of further assessments or contact,
19|including modified follow up options (see Section 7.1.2). Discontinuation of IP in itself is not
20|considered withdrawal of consent.
21|Withdrawal of consent must be ascertained and documented in writing by the Investigator who
22|must inform the AZ representative and document the withdrawal of consent in the eCRF and
23|medical records.
24|A patient who withdraws from the study will always be asked about the reason(s) and the presence
25|of any AE. The Investigator will follow up AEs reported outside of the clinical study.
26|If a patient withdraws from participation in the study, then his/her enrolment and randomisation
27|codes cannot be reused. Withdrawn patients will not be replaced. If the patient withdraws consent
28|for disclosure of future information, the sponsor may retain and continue to use any data collected
29|before such a withdrawal of consent.
30|To ensure validity of study data, it is very important to collect as much data as possible throughout
31|the study and especially vital status (dead or alive) at study closure (also for patients who have
32|withdrawn their informed consent). The Investigator will therefore attempt to collect information
