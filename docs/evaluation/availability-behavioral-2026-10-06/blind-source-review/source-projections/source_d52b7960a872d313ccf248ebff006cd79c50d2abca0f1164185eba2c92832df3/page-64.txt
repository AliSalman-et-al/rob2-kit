8|A 3
9|Informed consent process
10|The Investigator or his/her representative will explain the nature of the study to the subject or
11|his/her legally authorised representative and answer all questions regarding the study.
12|
13|Subjects must be informed that their participation is voluntary. Subjects or their legally authorised
14|representative will be required to sign a statement of informed consent that meets the requirements
15|of 21 CFR 50, local regulations, ICH guidelines, Health Insurance Portability and Accountability
16|Act (HIPAA) requirements, where applicable, and the IRB/IEC or study centre.
17|
18|The medical record must include a statement that written informed consent was obtained before
19|the subject was enrolled in the study and the date the written consent was obtained. The authorised
20|person obtaining the informed consent must also sign the ICF.
21|Subjects must be re-consented to the most current version of the ICF(s) during their participation
22|in the study.

29|A 4
30|Data protection
31|Each subject will be assigned a unique identifier by the sponsor. Any subject records or data sets
32|transferred to the sponsor will contain only the identifier; subject names or any information which
33|would make the subject identifiable will not be transferred.
34|The subject must be informed that his/her personal study-related data will be used by the sponsor
35|in accordance with local data protection law. The level of disclosure must also be explained to the
36|subject.
37|The subject must be informed that his/her medical records may be examined by Clinical Quality
38|Assurance auditors or other authorised personnel appointed by the sponsor, by appropriate
39|IRB/IEC members, and by inspectors from regulatory authorities.
