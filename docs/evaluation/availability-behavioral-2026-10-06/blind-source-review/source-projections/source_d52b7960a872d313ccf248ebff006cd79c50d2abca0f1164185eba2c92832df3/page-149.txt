13|Safety reporting and medical management
14|8.6.1
15|Reporting of serious adverse events
16|All SAEs have to be reported, whether or not considered causally related to the investigational
17|product, or to the study procedureHsI. All SAEs will be recorded in the eCRF.
18|If any SAE occurs in the course of the study, then Investigators or other site personnel inform the
19|appropriate AstraZeneca representatives within one day i.e., immediately but no later than 24
20|hours of when he or she becomes aware of it.
21|The designated AstraZeneca representative works with the Investigator to ensure that all the
22|necessary information is provided to the AstraZeneca Patient Safety data entry site within 1
23|calendar day of initial receipt for fatal and life-threatening events and within 5 calendar days of
24|initial receipt for all other SAEs.
25|For fatal or life-threatening adverse events where important or relevant information is missing,
26|active follow-up is undertaken immediately. Investigators or other site personnel inform
27|AstraZeneca representatives of any follow-up information on a previously reported SAE within
28|one calendar day i.e., immediately but no later than 24 hours of when he or she becomes aware
29|of it.
30|Once the Investigators or other site personnel indicate an AE is serious in the EDC system, an
31|automated email alert is sent to the designated AstraZeneca representative.
32|If the EDC system is not available, then the Investigator or other study site staff reports a SAE to
33|the appropriate AstraZeneca representative by telephone.
34|The AstraZeneca representative will advise the InvestigatorPstudy site staff how to proceed.
35|Investigators or other site personnel send relevant CRF modules by fax to the designated
36|AstraZeneca representative.
37|For further guidance on the definition of a SAE, see Appendix B of the Clinical Study Protocol.
38|8.6.1.1
39|Reporting of SAEs considered to be potential endpoints
40|In order to avoid unnecessary unblinding of efficacy endpoint events, certain SAEs which are also
41|potential endpoints Hi.e., fatal AEs and HF eventsI will not be reported to health authorities. Clinical
42|data for the above mentioned events will be recorded as AEsPSAEs as well as on separate event
43|forms in the eCRF. Recording of a suspected endpoint should be done within the same timeframes
44|as defined for SAEs Hsee Section 8.6.1I.
