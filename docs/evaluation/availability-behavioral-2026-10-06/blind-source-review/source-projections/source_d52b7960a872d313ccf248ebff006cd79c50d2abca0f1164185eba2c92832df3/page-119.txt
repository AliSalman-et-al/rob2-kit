7|STUDY DESIGN
8|4.1
9|Overall design
10|This is an international, multicentre, parallel-group, event-driven, randomised, double-blind, placebo-
11|controlled study in HFpEF patients, evaluating the effect of dapagliflozin 10 mg versus placebo, given
12|once daily in addition to background regional standard of care therapy, including treatments to control
13|co-morbidities, in reducing the composite of CV death or heart failure events.
14|For an overview of the study design see Figure 1, Section 1.3. For details on treatments given
15|during the study, see Section 6.1.
16|For details on what is included in the efficacy and safety endpoints, see Section 3 Objectives and
17|Endpoints.
18|Adult patients with HFpEF Hdefined for the purposes of this study as LVEF N40K and evidence of
19|structural heart diseaseI aged U40 years and with NYHA class II- IV who meet the inclusion
20|criteria, and none of the exclusion criteria, will be randomised in a 1D1 ratio to receive either
21|dapagliflozin 10 mg or placebo. Randomised treatment should be started as soon as possible and
22|within 24 hours after randomisation. It is estimated that approximately 11000 patients at
23|approximately 400-500 sites in 20-25 countries will be enrolled to reach approximately 6100
24|randomised patients Hsee Section 9.2I.
25|Study closure procedures will be initiated when the predetermined number of primary endpoints
26|are predicted to have occurred HnX1117I, i.e. the Primary Analysis Censoring Date HPACDI.
27|Patients should be scheduled for a Study Closure Visit HSCVI within 6 weeks of the PACD, which
28|can be extended if decided by Global Study Team. The anticipated total study duration is
29|approximately 39 months dependent on randomisation rate and event rate. The study duration, and
30|the number of patients, may be changed if the randomisation rate or the event rate is different than
31|anticipated. The study may be terminated early if a clear harmful effect of the study treatment is
32|detected during the DMC review, or due to DMC recommendations following pre-specified interim
33|analyses Hsee Section 9.5I.
34|Data on baseline characteristics, endpoints and AEs will be collected through a validated electronic
35|data capture HEDCI system with electronic case report forms HeCRFsI.
