15|Protocol Title:
16|An International, Double-blind, Randomised Placebo-Controlled Phase III Study to Evaluate
17|the Effect of Dapagliflozin on Reducing CV Death or Worsening Heart Failure in Patients
18|with Heart Failure with Preserved Ejection Fraction (HFpEF).
19|Rationale:
20|The prevalence of chronic heart failure HHFI continues to increase globally, and the annual global
21|economic burden Hseveral hundred billion dollars in 2012I will increase as the population ages.
22|Approximately half of all heart failure patients have heart failure with preserved ejection fraction
23|HHFpEFI representing a particularly significant unmet need given that no approved
24|pharmacotherapy exists specifically for this condition. Patients with HFpEF generally receive
25|diuretic treatment for symptom relief, and should receive guideline recommended therapies for
26|concomitant diseases such as hypertension. Recent data from cardiovascular HCVI outcome trials
27|of SGLT2 inhibitors Hempagliflozin and canagliflozinI and real world studies Hincluding patients
28|treated with dapagliflozinI indicate that treatment with SGLT2 inhibitors can reduce the risk of CV
29|death and hospitalisation due to HF in patients with Type 2 Diabetes HT2DI overall and in patients
30|with T2D and concomitant HF. Limitations associated with the randomised clinical trials as well
31|as the observational studies are that only patients with T2D were studied, and that the proportion
32|of patients with HFrEF and HFpEF, respectively, is unknown. This study will test the hypothesis
33|that dapagliflozin will reduce the composite of CV death and HF events Hhospitalisation for HF or
34|urgent HF visitI in patients with HF and preserved systolic function HLVEF N40KI, with or without
35|T2D.
