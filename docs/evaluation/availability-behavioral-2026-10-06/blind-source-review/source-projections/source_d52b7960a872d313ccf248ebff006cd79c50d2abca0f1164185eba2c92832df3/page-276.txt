22|Data
23|The anchor-based analysis of change from baseline KCCQ-TSS at 8 months in different
24|categories of change from baseline PGIS at 8 monthsO is presented in Table A2. As this
25|analysis is done on blinded study data and only includes patients with observed values for
26|both KCCQ-TSS and PGIS at 8 months (patients who died and all other patients with missing
27|data are excluded)O the EmeanF is selected as a representation of the average of a group. This
28|anchor-based analysis indicates that small or moderate improvement corresponds to a mean

32|corresponds to a mean decrease in KCCQ-TSS of 5 points. It is important to note that the
33|group of patients who were categoriIed as being EstableF in terms of their HF symptoms at 8
34|months had a mean increase in KCCQ-TSS of almost 5 points.
