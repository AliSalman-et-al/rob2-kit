14|pathophysiology in HFpEF is abnormal pressure coupling between the left ventricle and aorta, and

20|lowering blood pressure in patients with T2D HOtt et al 2017I. This prior work suggests that
21|selecting the 10-mg dose of dapagliflozin is reasonable from a mechanistic perspective to
22|demonstrate a clinical effect.

24|End of study definition
25|The end of study is defined as the last expected visitPcontact of the last subject undergoing the
26|study.
27|The study may be terminated at individual study sites if the study procedures are not being
28|performed according to GCP, or if no patients are recruited. Patients from terminated sites will
29|have the opportunity to be transferred to another site to continue the study. AstraZeneca may also
30|terminate the entire study prematurely if concerns for safety arise within this study or in any other
31|study with dapagliflozin, or due to recommendation by the DMC. Regardless of the reason for
32|termination, all data required by the protocol at the time of discontinuation of follow-up will be
33|collected. In terminating the study, the Sponsor will ensure that adequate consideration is given to
34|the protection of the patientsO interests.
