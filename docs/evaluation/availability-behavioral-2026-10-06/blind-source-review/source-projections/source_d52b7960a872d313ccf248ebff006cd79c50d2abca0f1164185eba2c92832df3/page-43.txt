32|8.3
33|Efficacy assessments
34|8.3.1
35|Efficacy event capture
36|Efficacy events (i.e. death, hospitalisation or urgent visits for HF) will be collected by site
37|personnel according to the study visit schedule. All potential efficacy events should be recorded
38|as an AE and on additional event modules in the eCRF. If the potential efficacy event fulfils SAE
39|criteria (see Appendix B 2) the site is to record and report these events to the sponsor or designee
40|within timelines described in Section 8.6.
41|NYHA classification will be done by the Investigators and recorded in the eCRF. PROs will be
42|collected for all patients throughout the study period via a hand-held electronic device. All-cause
43|hospitalisations will be derived from SAE reports.
44|8.3.2
45|Efficacy event adjudication
46|A Clinical Events Adjudication (CEA) Committee will be established for this trial and adjudicate
47|primary efficacy events in accordance with adjudication criteria detailed in the CEA charter.
48|Events to be adjudicated include components of the primary efficacy endpoint: deaths,
49|hospitalisation for HF, and urgent HF visits. All deaths will be adjudicated to determine if they are
50|CV or non-CV deaths. All adjudication will be done on an ongoing basis throughout the trial.
