6|Systemic
7|Search
8|To ensure trials beyond DARE-19 and DELIVER were not missed, a systemic search via PubMed
9|and EMBASE will be conducted of
10|x Randomized, placebo-controlled trials of SGLT2 inhibitors in COVID-19
11|x Published between March 1st, 2020 to August 1, 2022
12|
13|Rationale
14|DARE-19 randomized non-critically ill patients with one or more cardiometabolic risk factors
15|(including T2D, HTN, ASCVD, HF or CKD) hospitalized with COVID-19 to dapagliflozin versus
16|placebo, with one of the primary outcomes being respiratory/ cardiovascular/ kidney organ failure or
17|death from any cause. DELIVER randomized patients with HF and LVEF above 40% to dapagliflozin
18|or placebo, and due to the time course of the trial had many patients experiencing COVID-19 related
19|hospitalizations and deaths. Neither trial was adequately powered to assess the effects of
20|dapagliflozin on all-cause mortality, and specific end-organ complications. This pre-specified meta-
21|analysis will allow for greater power to evaluate the effects of dapagliflozin on a range of COVID-19
22|related clinical endpoints.
23|Overall
24|Aim
25|Using study-level published data from DARE-19 and participant-level data from DELIVER, we aim
26|to estimate the effect of SGLT2 inhibitors on all-cause mortality and specific end-organ
27|complications overall, and in clinically-relevant subgroups
28|Primary
29|Endpoint
30|COVID-19 related death (this includes COVID-19 related deaths in DELIVER and all deaths in
31|DARE-19)

53|Analysis
54|x Intention-to-treat analyses from both trials will be considered and include all randomized
