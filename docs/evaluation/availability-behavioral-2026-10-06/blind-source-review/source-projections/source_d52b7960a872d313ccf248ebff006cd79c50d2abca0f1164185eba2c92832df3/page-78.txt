8|Appendix E Handling of Human Biological Samples
9|E 1
10|Chain of custody of biological samples
11|A full chain of custody is maintained for all samples throughout their lifecycle.
12|The Investigator at each centre keeps full traceability of collected biological samples from the
13|subjects while in storage at the centre until shipment or disposal (where appropriate).
14|The sample receiver keeps full traceability of the samples while in storage and during use until
15|used or disposed of or until further shipment and keeps documentation of receipt of arrival.
16|AstraZeneca will keep oversight of the entire life cycle through internal procedures, monitoring of
17|study sites, auditing or process checks, and contractual requirements of external laboratory
18|providers
19|Samples retained for further use will be stored in the AZ-assigned biobanks and will be registered
20|by the AstraZeneca Biobank Team during the entire life cycle.
21|If required, AstraZeneca will ensure that remaining biological samples are returned to the site
22|according to local regulations or at the end of the retention period, whichever is the sooner.
23|E 2
24|Withdrawal of Informed Consent for donated biological samples
25|If a subject withdraws consent to the use of donated biological samples, the samples will be
26|disposed of/destroyed, and the action documented. If samples are already analysed, AstraZeneca
27|is not obliged to destroy the results of this research.
28|As collection of the biological sample(s) is an integral part of the study, then the subject is
29|withdrawn from further study participation.
