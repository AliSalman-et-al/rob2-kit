38|8.5.1.4 Capture of Major hypoglycaemic events
39|A major hypoglycemic event is defined as an event that is characterized by altered mental andPor
40|physical status, any symptoms of severe impairment in consciousness or behavior, that require
41|external assistance of another person for treatment of hypoglycemia and recovery, to actively
42|administer carbohydrates, glucagon, or take other corrective actions.
43|Plasma glucose concentrations may not be available during a hypoglycaemic event, but
44|neurological recovery following the corrective actions is considered sufficient evidence that the
45|event was induced by a low plasma glucose concentration. Major hypoglycaemic episodes will be
46|recorded in the eCRF as an AE and on an additional eCRF page.
