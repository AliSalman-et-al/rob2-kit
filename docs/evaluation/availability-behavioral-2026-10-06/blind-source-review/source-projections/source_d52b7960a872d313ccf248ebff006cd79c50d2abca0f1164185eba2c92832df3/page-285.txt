8|INTRODUCTION
9|DELIVER is an international, multicentre, parallel group, event-driven, randomized, double-
10|blind trial in patients with chronic heart failure and left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF)
11|>40%, comparing the effect of dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily, vs. placebo, in addition to
12|standard of care. Patients with or without diabetes, with signs and symptoms of heart failure, a
13|LVEF >40%, elevation in natriuretic peptides and evidence of structural heart disease are
14|eligible. The primary endpoint is time-to-first cardiovascular death or worsening heart failure
15|event (heart failure hospitalization or urgent heart failure visit), and will be assessed in dual
16|primary analyses ± the full population and in those with LVEF <60%. The study is event-driven
17|and will target 1117 primary events. A total of 6,263 patients have been randomized.
18|
19|The DELIVER executive committee has developed this academic statistical analysis plan
20|(ASAP) that describes pre-specified analyses that were not described in the DELIVER regulatory
21|SAP (rSAP). General principles outlined in the regulatory SAP will be followed unless specified
22|otherwise here. This document is meant to supplement and complement the regulatory SAP and
23|delineate all analyses that were pre-specified prior to database lock. When relevant, analyses will
24|be conducted based on the pooled DAPA-HF and DELIVER dataset to examine the effects of
25|dapagliflozin in a broad range of patients with HF.

27|2.
28|CLINICAL ENDPOINTS OF INTEREST
29|In addition to the efficacy and safety variables listed in the rSAP, the effect of dapagliflozin on
30|the following endpoints will be explored. These events that are imbalanced between arms may be
31|analyzed as time-to-event to better understand the time course. All endpoints will be assessed in
32|the full cohort and in the LVEF < 60% subgroup. These include:
33|
34|x Days alive and out of the hospital
35|x Quality of life-adjusted days alive and out of the hospital
36|x Investigator reported vs. CEC-adjudicated endpoints
37|x Time to onset of benefit of dapagliflozin
38|x New diuretic initiation, discontinuation, and dose changes
39|x New onset atrial fibrillation
40|x In the T2D subgroup, new glucose lowering therapy initiation and changes in insulin dose
41|(in those on insulin at baseline)
42|x In the non-T2D subgroup, new diagnosis of diabetes
43|x Signs and symptoms of HF
44|x Patient Global Impression of Severity
45|
46|x Target risk factor control (for blood pressure, smoking, antiplatelet/anticoagulant
47|therapy)
48|x Cardiac ischemic events including myocardial infarction, unstable angina, unplanned
49|coronary revascularization, and stroke
50|x Hyperkalemia as a reported adverse event and initiation of new potassium-lowering
51|therapy
52|x Acute kidney injury as a reported adverse event and initiation of dialysis
53|x Anemia and requirement for blood transfusion as reported adverse events
54|x Gout as an adverse event and initiation of new uric acid-lowering therapy
55|x KCCQ Overall Summary Score at 1, 4 and 8 months
