23|Protocol Title:
24|An International, Double-blind, Randomised Placebo-Controlled Phase III Study
25|to Evaluate the Effect of Dapagliflozin on Reducing CV Death or Worsening Heart Failure
26|in Patients with Heart Failure with Preserved Ejection Fraction (HFpEF).
27|
28|Rationale:
29|The prevalence of chronic heart failure (HF) continues to increase globally, and the annual global
30|economic burden (several hundred billion dollars in 2012) will increase as the population ages.
31|Approximately half of all heart failure patients have heart failure with preserved ejection fraction
32|(HFpEF) representing a particularly significant unmet need given that no approved
33|pharmacotherapy exists specifically for this condition. Patients with HFpEF generally receive
34|diuretic treatment for symptom relief, and should receive guideline recommended therapies for
35|concomitant diseases such as hypertension. Recent data from cardiovascular (CV) outcome trials
36|of SGLT2 inhibitors (empagliflozin and canagliflozin) and real world studies (including patients
37|treated with dapagliflozin) indicate that treatment with SGLT2 inhibitors can reduce the risk of
38|CV death and hospitalisation due to HF in patients with Type 2 Diabetes (T2D) overall and in
39|patients with T2D and concomitant HF. Limitations associated with the randomised clinical trials
40|as well as the observational studies are that only patients with T2D were studied, and that the
41|proportion of patients with HFrEF and HFpEF, respectively, is unknown. This study will test the
42|hypothesis that dapagliflozin will reduce the composite of CV death and HF events (hospitalisation
43|for HF or urgent HF visit) in patients with HF and preserved systolic function (LVEF >40%), with
44|or without T2D.
