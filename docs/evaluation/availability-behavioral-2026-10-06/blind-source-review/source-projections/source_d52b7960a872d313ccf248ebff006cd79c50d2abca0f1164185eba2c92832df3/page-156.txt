9|Data monitoring committee (DMC)
10|An independent data monitoring committee HDMCI will be appointed and will report to the
11|Executive Committee. The DMC will be responsible for safeguarding the interests of the patients
12|in the outcome study by assessing the safety of the intervention during the study. The DMC will
13|have access to the individual treatment codes and be able to merge these with the collected study
14|data while the study is ongoing. A charter will be prepared to detail precise roles and
15|responsibilities and procedures of the DMC.
