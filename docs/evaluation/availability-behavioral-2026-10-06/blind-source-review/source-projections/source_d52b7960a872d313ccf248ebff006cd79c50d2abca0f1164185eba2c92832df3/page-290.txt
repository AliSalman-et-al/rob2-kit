6|7. META-ANALYSIS OF SGLT2 INHIBITOR HFPEF TRIALS AND OTHER SGLT2
7|INHIBITOR TRIALS
8|A meta-analysis will be performed using available phase 3/4 published trials of other sodium±
9|glucose cotransporter 2 inhibitor therapies in HFpEF, including but not limited to EMPEROR-
10|Preserved.
11|
12|Systemic
13|Search
14|To ensure trials beyond EMPEROR-Preserved and DELIVER were not missed, a systemic search via
15|PubMed and EMBASE will be conducted of
16|x Randomized, placebo-controlled CV and kidney outcomes trials of SGLT2 inhibitors
17|x Published between January 1, 2015 to July 1, 2022
18|x Only studies including >1,000 patients with HF and LVEF >40%
19|Rationale
20|Both EMPEROR-Preserved and DELIVER were similarly designed in evaluating patients with HF,
21|an LVEF above 40%, and elevated natriuretic peptides. Neither trial was powered for mortality or
22|kidney disease outcomes. This pre-specified meta-analysis of the 2 largest trials of HFmrEF and
23|HFpEF will allow for greater power to evaluate a broad range of clinical endpoints and within
24|subgroups of interest than either trial could provide alone.
25|Overall
26|Aim
27|Using study-level published data from EMPEROR-Preserved and participant-level data from
28|DELIVER, we aim to estimate the effect of SGLT2 inhibitors on cardiovascular events, kidney
29|events, and mortality outcomes overall, and in clinically-relevant subgroups
30|Primary
31|Endpoint
32|Time from randomization to the occurrence of the composite of death adjudicated as CV cause or
33|unplanned HF hospitalization
34|Secondary
35|Endpoints
36|x Time from randomization to the occurrence of the composite of death adjudicated as CV
37|cause or a worsening HF event (including either unplanned hospitalization or urgent HF visit
38|requiring IV therapy)
39|x Total number of worsening HF events and cardiovascular death
40|x Time from randomization to the occurrence of deaths adjudicated as CV cause
41|x Time from randomization to death from any cause
42|x Time from randomization to renal composite outcome (50% or higher sustained decline in
43|eGFR, end stage kidney disease, or renal death)
44|x Proportion of patients with clinically meaningful deterioration (5 point or greater worsening),
45|aQG VPaOO ( 5 SRLQW), PRGHUaWH ( 10 SRLQW), aQG OaUJH ( 15 SRLQW) LPSURYHPent in KCCQ-
46|TSS, CSS, OSS
47|Subgroups
48|x LVEF (<50%, 50 WR <60%, 60%)
49|x With or without diabetes
50|x Use of no use of ACEi/ARB/ARNI at baseline
51|x Use and no use of MRA at baseline
52|x Age ( 70 aQG <70 years), sex (male, female), race (White, Black, Asian, Other), BMI (<30
53|aQG 30 NJ/P2), eGFR ( 60 and <60mL/min/1.73m2), systolic blood pressure, history of
54|AF/AFL, hospitalization for HF within 12 months, NYHA class (II and III/IV)
55|Statistical
56|Analysis
57|x Fixed effects model
58|x Only intention-to-treat analyses from both trials will be considered and include all randomized
59|participants
60|x All effect sizes will be extracted as point estimates (95% CI). For the time-to-first event
61|endpoints, Cox proportional hazards models will be used for hazard ratio (HR) and 95% CI.
