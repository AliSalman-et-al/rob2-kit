7|Genetic research plan and procedures
8|Selection of genetic research population
9|Study selection record
10|All subjects will be asked to participate in this genetic research. Participation is voluntary and if a
11|subject decline to participate there will be no penalty or loss of benefit. The subject will not be
12|excluded from any aspect of the main study.
13|Inclusion criteria
14|
15|For inclusion in this genetic research, subjects must fulfil all of the inclusion criteria
16|described in the main body of the Clinical Study Protocol and Provide informed consent
17|for the genetic sampling and analyses.
18|Exclusion criteria
19|Exclusion from this genetic research may be for any of the exclusion criteria specified in the
20|main study or any of the followingD
21|
22|Previous allogeneic bone marrow transplant
23|
24|

26|collection
27|Withdrawal of consent for genetic research:
28|Subjects may withdraw from this genetic research at any time, independent of any decision
29|concerning participation in other aspects of the main study. Voluntary withdrawal will not
30|prejudice further treatment. Procedures for withdrawal are outlined in Section 7 of the main
31|Clinical Study Protocol.
32|Collection of samples for genetic research
33|The blood sample for genetic research will be obtained from the subjects at Visit 2. Although DNA
34|is stable, early sample collection is preferred to avoid introducing bias through excluding subjects
35|who may withdraw due to an adverse event HAEI, such subjects would be important to include in
36|any genetic analysis. If for any reason the sample is not drawn at Visit 2, it may be taken at any
37|visit until the last study visit. Only one sample should be collected per subject for genetics during
38|the study. Samples will be collected, labelled, stored, and shipped as detailed in the Laboratory
39|Manual.
40|Coding and storage of DNA samples
41|The processes adopted for the coding and storage of samples for genetic analysis are important to
42|maintain subject confidentiality. Samples will be stored for a maximum of 15 years, from the date
43|of last subject last visit, after which they will be destroyed. DNA is a finite resource that is used up
44|during analyses. Samples will be stored and used until no further analyses are possible or the
45|maximum storage time has been reached.
