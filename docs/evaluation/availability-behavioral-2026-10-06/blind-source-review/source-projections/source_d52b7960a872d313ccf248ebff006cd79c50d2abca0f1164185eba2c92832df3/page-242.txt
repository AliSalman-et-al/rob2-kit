47|Primary Analysis Censoring Date
48|The executive committee and AstraZeneca will monitor the accrual of endpoint events and
49|when appropriate define the PACD at which time at least the pre-defined target number of
50|1117 events for the primary composite endpoint is expected to have occurred. The study sites
51|will be instructed to plan for SCV to be performed within 6 weeGs after PACDO which can be
52|extended if decided by the Global Study Team. Patients will stop taGing IP at the SCV.
53|Analyses of efficacy endpoint events will include events with onset on or prior to PACD.
54|Event free patients who have not been prematurely censored due to incomplete information
55|(see Section 3.1) will be censored at PACD. HF events and deaths with onset after PACD will
56|also be adjudicated.
