37|9.5
38|Interim analyses
39|An interim analysis is planned to be performed including approximately 67% of the target number
40|of patients with adjudicated primary endpoint events. There will in principle be one planned
41|interim analysis for efficacy, with the possibility of the DMC to conduct subsequent interim
42|analysis if they deem necessary. The significance level for final analysis will be determined by the
43|Haybittle-Peto function based on the actual number of interim analyses. The interim analysis will
44|assess superiority of dapagliflozin to placebo. The interim analysis will have a nominal two-sided
45|alpha level of 0.2%. At the interim analysis, the primary composite endpoint will be tested first at
46|the specified alpha level. If superiority is achieved for the primary endpoint, then the superiority
47|of dapagliflozin to placebo on CV deaths will be tested at a two-sided level of 0.2%. If CV death
48|is significant, then an action is triggered whereby the DMC will evaluate the totality of the efficacy
49|data and safety data, to determine if benefit is unequivocal and overwhelming such that the DMC
50|recommends ending the study.
