20|Appendix E
21|Handling of Human Biological Samples
22|E 1
23|Chain of custody of biological samples
24|A full chain of custody is maintained for all samples throughout their lifecycle.
25|The Investigator at each centre keeps full traceability of collected biological samples from the
26|subjects while in storage at the centre until shipment or disposal Hwhere appropriateI.
27|The sample receiver keeps full traceability of the samples while in storage and during use until
28|used or disposed of or until further shipment and keeps documentation of receipt of arrival.
29|AstraZeneca will keep oversight of the entire life cycle through internal procedures, monitoring of
30|study sites, auditing or process checks, and contractual requirements of external laboratory
31|providers
32|Samples retained for further use will be stored in the AZ-assigned biobanks and will be registered
33|by the AstraZeneca Biobank Team during the entire life cycle.
34|If required, AstraZeneca will ensure that remaining biological samples are returned to the site
35|according to local regulations or at the end of the retention period, whichever is the sooner.
36|E 2
37|Withdrawal of Informed Consent for donated biological samples
38|If a subject withdraws consent to the use of donated biological samples, the samples will be
39|disposed ofPdestroyed, and the action documented. If samples are already analysed, AstraZeneca
40|is not obliged to destroy the results of this research.
