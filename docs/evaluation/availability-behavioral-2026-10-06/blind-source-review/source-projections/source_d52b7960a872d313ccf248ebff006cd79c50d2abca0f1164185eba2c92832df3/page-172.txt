24|Genetic variation may impact a subjectOs response to therapy, susceptibility to, and severity and
25|progression of disease. Variable response to therapy may be due to genetic determinants that impact
26|drug absorption, distribution, metabolism, and excretionR mechanism of action of the drugR disease
27|aetiologyR andPor molecular subtype of the disease being treated. Therefore, where local regulations
28|and IRBPIEC allow, a blood sample will be collected for DNA analysis from consenting subjects.
29|AstraZeneca intends to collect and store DNA for genetic research to explore how genetic
30|variations may affect clinical parameters, risk and prognosis of diseases, and the response to
31|medications. Genetic research may lead to better understanding of diseases, better diagnosis of
32|diseases or other improvements in health care and to the discovery of new diagnostics, treatments
33|or medications.
34|In addition, collection of DNA samples from populations with well described clinical
35|characteristics may lead to improvements in the design and interpretation of clinical trials and,
36|possibly, to genetically guided treatment strategies.
37|Genetic research may consist of the analysis of the structure of the subject[s DNA, i.e. the entire
38|genome.
39|The results of genetic analyses may be reported in the clinical study report HCSRI or in a separate
40|study summary.
41|The sponsor will store the DNA samples in a secure storage space with adequate measures to
42|protect confidentiality.
43|The samples will be retained while research on heart failure with preserved ejection fraction
44|continues but no longer than 15 years or other period as per local requirements.
