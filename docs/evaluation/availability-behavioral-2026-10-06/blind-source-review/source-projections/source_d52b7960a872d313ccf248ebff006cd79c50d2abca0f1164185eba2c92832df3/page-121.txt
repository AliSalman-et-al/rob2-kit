32|Justification for dose
33|The 10 mg dose of dapagliflozin has a well-characterised efficacy and safety profile in the T2D
34|clinical development program and is the recommended dose in the majority of countries
35|worldwide.
36|From a pharmacokinetic perspective, the currently approved dapagliflozin dose of 10 mg once
37|daily is appropriate for use in patients with HFpEF. Slightly higher systemic exposure to
38|dapagliflozin is expected in HFpEF patients when symptomatic, based on the dual renal and hepatic
39|metabolism of dapagliflozin and the lower perfusion of these organs in this patient group. However,
40|the increase in systemic exposure of 10 mg dapagliflozin is not anticipated to warrant dose
41|adjustment in HF patients. Moreover, the anticipated slightly higher systemic exposure to
42|dapagliflozin is likely to be beneficial in HF patients, by compensating for the reduced renal
43|perfusion and consequently lower renal glucose and sodium filtered loads in these patients. Doses
44|lower than 10 mg are therefore unlikely to provide as much benefit to patients with HF as the 10-
