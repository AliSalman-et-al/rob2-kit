7|Administration of electronic PROs
8|Each site must allocate the responsibility for the administration of the ePROs to a specific
9|individual and, if possible, assign a backup person to cover if that individual is absent. A key aspect
10|of study success is to have high PRO compliance. Therefore, it is essential to follow SoA and that
11|sites make sure the device is charged and fully functional at all times in order to minimize missing
12|data.
13|It is important that the site staff explains the value and relevance of PRO dataD to hear directly from
14|patients how they feel. The following best practice guidelines should be followedD
