10|C. Undetermined Stroke
11|Undetermined stroke is defined as an acute episode of focal or global neurological dysfunction caused
12|by presumed brain, spinal cord, or retinal vascular injury as a result of haemorrhage or infarction but
13|with insufficient information to allow categorization as either ischemic or haemorrhagic.
