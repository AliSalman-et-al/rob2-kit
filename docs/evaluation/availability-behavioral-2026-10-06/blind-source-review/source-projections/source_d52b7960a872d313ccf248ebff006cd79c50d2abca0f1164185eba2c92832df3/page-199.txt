50|Definitions
51|1.2.1
52|Primary analysis censoring date
53|The executive committee and AstraZeneca will monitor the accrual of endpoint events and
54|when appropriate define the primary analysis censoring date (PACD) at which time at least
55|the pre-defined target number of 844 events for the primary composite endpoint is expected to
56|have occurred. The study sites will be instructed to plan for study closure visits to be
57|performed after PACD.
58|Analyses of efficacy endpoint events will include events with onset on or prior to PACD.
59|Event free patients who have not been prematurely censored due to incomplete information
60|(see Section 3.1) will be censored at PACD. HF events and deaths with onset after PACD will
61|also be adjudicated.
