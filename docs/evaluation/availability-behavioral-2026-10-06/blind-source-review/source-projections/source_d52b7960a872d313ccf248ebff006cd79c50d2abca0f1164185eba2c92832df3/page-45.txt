27|EuroQoL five-dimensional five-level questionnaire (EQ-5D-5L)
28|The EQ-5D-5L is a self-reported questionnaire that is used to derive a standardized measure of
29|health status, also referred to as a utility score. EQ-5D-5L utility scores are widely accepted by
30|reimbursement authorities and will be used to support health economic evaluations.

32|Administration of electronic PROs
33|Each site must allocate the responsibility for the administration of the ePROs to a specific
34|individual and, if possible, assign a backup person to cover if that individual is absent.
35|A key aspect of study success is to have high PRO compliance. Therefore, it is essential to follow
36|SoA and that sites make sure the device is charged and fully functional at all times in order to
37|minimize missing data.
