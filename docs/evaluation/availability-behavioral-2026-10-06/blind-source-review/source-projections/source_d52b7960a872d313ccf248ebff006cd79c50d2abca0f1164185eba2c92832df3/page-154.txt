27|9.4.1.3
28|Subgroup analysis
29|Subgroup variables for the primary efficacy endpoint include demography, baseline disease
30|characteristics, baseline concomitant medications and others. Cox proportional hazard model
31|stratified for T2D with factors for treatment group, the subgroup variable and the interaction
32|between treatment and subgroup will be used to examine treatment effects within relevant
33|subgroups separately. A test of interaction between randomised treatment group and the subgroup
34|variable will be performed in each Cox model. The p-values for the subgroup analyses will not be
35|adjusted for multiple comparisons as the tests are exploratory and will be interpreted descriptively.
36|Treatment differences with 95K confidence intervals will be reported for each subgroup. HRs and
37|CIs for overall analysis and subgroups will be presented with forest plots as well. Further details
38|of the subgroup analysis, including the list of subgroup variables, will be provided in the SAP.
