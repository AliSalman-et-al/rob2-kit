15|Statistical methods
16|This study is event-driven with a target of 1117 patients with a primary endpoint event. The primary
17|objective of the study is to determine the superiority of dapagliflozin versus placebo, when added to
18|standard of care, in reducing the composite of CV death and HF events Hhospitalisation for HF or urgent
19|HF visitI. Two hypotheses will be tested simultaneously Hi.e, dual primary analysesI for this primary
20|objectiveD H1I in the full population and in H2I an LVEF J60K subpopulation, with alpha allocated to
21|each test. The final alpha split will be defined in the SAP prior to the interim analysis. It is anticipated
22|that at least 70K of the events Hi.e. approximately 780 eventsI will be available for the LVEF J60K
23|subpopulation. To illustrate, assuming a true hazard ratio HHRI of 0.80 between dapagliflozin and
24|placebo, a two-sided alpha of 1.5K would yield a power of 90K for the full population and a two-sided
25|alpha of 2.4K would yield a power of 80K for the LVEF J60K subpopulation.
26|Based on above assumption and ongoing blinded monitoring of events accrual, approximately 6100
27|patients are estimated to provide the required number of primary events in the full population during
28|an anticipated recruitment period up to 29 months and followed until the pre-specified number of
29|primary events has occurred. Randomisation will be stratified by presence or absence of Type 2
30|Diabetes HT2DI.
31|All patients who have been randomised to study treatment will be included in the Full Analysis Set
32|HFASI irrespective of their protocol adherence and continued participation in the study. The primary
33|variable is the time to first event included in the primary composite endpoint. The primary analysis will
34|be based on the intention to treat HITTI principle using the FAS, including events occurring on or prior
35|to the primary analysis censoring date HPACDI, confirmed by adjudication.
36|In the analysis of the primary composite endpoint, treatments Hdapagliflozin versus placeboI will
37|be compared using a Cox proportional hazards model with a factor for treatment group, stratified
38|by type 2 diabetes HT2DI status at randomisation. The p-value, hazard ratio and 95K confidence
39|interval will be reported.
