7|Procedures for handling of randomized not eligible patients
8|If a patient is randomised and later found not eligible, the Investigator should immediately inform
9|the AstraZeneca representative, who will report the protocol deviation to the AstraZeneca Study
10|Physician.
11|Study treatment must be discontinued in all cases where continued treatment is deemed to pose a safety
12|risk to the patient. Regardless of whether study treatment is discontinued or not, the patient should
13|continue hisPher participation in the study for follow- up of endpoints and other protocol-defined study
14|procedures until the end of the study. Consistent with the intention-to-treat principle, all randomised
15|patients are included in the efficacy analysis according to randomised treatment assignment. The
16|AstraZeneca Study Physician must ensure that the protocol deviation and the rationale for the decision
17|to discontinue or continue study treatment are appropriately documented.
