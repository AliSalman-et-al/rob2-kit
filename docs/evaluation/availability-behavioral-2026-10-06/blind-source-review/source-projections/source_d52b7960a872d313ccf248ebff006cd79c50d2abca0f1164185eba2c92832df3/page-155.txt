17|9.4.3
18|Methods for multiplicity control
19|A closed testing procedure including a pre-specified hierarchical ordering of the primary and
20|secondary endpoints will be utilised. The Type I error will be controlled at an overall two-sided
21|5K level for multiplicity across primary and secondary endpoints and in consideration of the
22|planned interim analysis. Qith one interim analysis at 67K of events Hsee Section 9.5I the two-
23|sided significance level in final analysis, c, will be 4.980K. Statistical significance will be assessed
24|in two branches in a pre-specified order of the endpoints and populations which is further described
25|in the SAP. The significance level c, will be split for the two primary analyses, denoted c1 and c2.
26|If either of the tests of the primary endpoint in the full study population and for LVEF J60K
27|subpopulation is significant at respective levels c1 and c2, the next hypothesis in the respective
28|branch sequence will be tested at the same significance level. The exact split of alpha will be
29|documented in an updated SAP before the interim analysis. If all hypotheses in one arm are
30|rejected, the alpha will be recycled to the other branch.
31|9.5
32|Interim analyses
33|An interim analysis is planned to be performed including approximately 67K of the target number
34|of patients with adjudicated primary endpoint events Happroximately 748 eventsI. There will in
35|principle be one planned interim analysis for efficacy, with the possibility of the DMC to conduct
36|subsequent interim analysis if they deem necessary. The significance level for final analysis will
37|be determined by the Haybittle-Peto function based on the actual number of interim analyses. The
38|interim analysis will assess superiority of dapagliflozin to placebo. The interim analysis will have
39|a nominal two-sided alpha level of 0.2K. At the interim analysis, the primary composite endpoint
40|will be tested in the full study population at the specified alpha level. If superiority is achieved for
41|the primary endpoint, then the superiority of dapagliflozin to placebo on CV deaths will be tested
42|in the full study population at a two-sided level of 0.2K. If CV death is significant, then an action
43|is triggered whereby the DMC will evaluate the totality of the efficacy data and safety data, to
44|determine if benefit is unequivocal and overwhelming such that the DMC recommends ending the
45|study.
