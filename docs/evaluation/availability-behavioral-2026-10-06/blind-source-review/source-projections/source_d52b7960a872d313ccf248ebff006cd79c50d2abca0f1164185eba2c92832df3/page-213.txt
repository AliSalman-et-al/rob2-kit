12|Missing data and informative censoring
13|The time-to-event analysis using the Cox regression depends on the assumption of non-
14|informative or ignorable censoringH corresponding to the missing-at-random assumption. The
15|missing data in this context are patients who are prematurely censored due to WoCH LTFU or
16|otherwise incomplete follow-up of endpoints. The amount of missing data will be described
17|egH in terms of the number of patients and patient time with incomplete follow-up as described
18|in Section 4.1.5.
19|Patient retention and follow-up are at the forefront of study planning and conductH and the
20|amount of incomplete follow-up is expected to be small. To assess the impact of missing data
