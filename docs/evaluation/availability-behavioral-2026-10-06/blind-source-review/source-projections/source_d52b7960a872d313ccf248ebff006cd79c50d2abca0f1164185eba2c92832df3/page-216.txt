7|Handling of missing data
8|The number of patients with missing vital status at 8 months is expected to be negligible. If
9|some patients are LTFU or patients who withdrew consent have unknown vital statusH the
10|main analysis will be done with these patients assigned the worst ranks (same as deaths).
11|In the context of analysing the composite ranked endpoint as described aboveH missing data
12|may arise when patients miss the 8-month KCCQ assessment while remaining in the study
13|during the 8-month assessment windowH or when patients withdraw consent from the study
14|prior to 8 months. If a patient is known to have died prior to the 8-month assessmentH the
15|patient is considered to have a non-missing composite outcome and will be handled as
16|described above (assigned the worst rank). OtherwiseH patients who are alive at 8 months and
17|have missing baseline or 8-month KCCQ assessments will have their missing TSS imputed
18|using the multiple imputation (MI) methodology as follows.
19|Missing TSS values at baseline or at 8 months will be imputed under the Missing at Random
20|(MAR) assumption. The imputation will be done using a predictive mean matching multiple
21|imputation model and a method of Fully Conditional Specification as implemented in the SAS
22|Procedure MI (FCS statement). The predictive mean matching method ensures that the
23|imputed values remain in the permissible range of the TSS values. The imputation model will
24|include the treatment groupH T2D randomiDation stratumH TSS at baselineH month 1H 4H and 8H
25|and three auxiliary binary variables representing occurrences of any HF events in the intervals
26|from randomiDation to 1 monthH from 1 to 4 monthsH and from 4 to 8 monthsH respectively.
27|Occurrences of HF events will be determined based on the investigator-reported potential HF
28|events. Auxiliary variables related to HF events are included in the imputation model to
29|improve the imputation accuracyH because the occurrence of HF events is associated with
30|Cuality of life assessed by KCCQ.
31|The number of closest observations used to sample an imputed value by the predictive mean
32|matching method will be 5 (SAS default setting).
33|Each imputed dataset will be analysed using the methods described in the RHypothesis
34|testingS and REstimation of treatment effectS sub-sections above. The results from multiple
35|imputed datasets will be combined using RubinOs rule as implemented in the SAS Procedure
36|MIANALYZE.
