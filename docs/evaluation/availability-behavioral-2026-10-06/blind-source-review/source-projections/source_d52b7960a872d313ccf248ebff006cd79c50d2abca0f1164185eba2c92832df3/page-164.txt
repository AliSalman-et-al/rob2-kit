17|Data Monitoring Committee (DMC)
18|An independent DMC will be appointed and will report to the Executive Committee. The DMC
19|will be responsible for safeguarding the interests of the patients in the outcome study by assessing
20|the safety of the intervention during the study, and for reviewing the overall conduct of the study.
21|The DMC will have access to the individual treatment codes and be able to merge these with the
22|collected study data while the study is ongoing. A DMC charter will be prepared to detail precise
23|roles and responsibilities and procedures to ensure maintenance of the blinding and integrity of the
24|study in the review of accumulating data and interactions with the Executive Committee.
25|Clinical Event Adjudication (CEA) Committee
26|The role of the CEA committee is to independently review, interpret and adjudicate potential
27|endpoints that are experienced by the patients. Endpoints will be identified preliminary by the
28|investigators, and also by AZ personnel or in the CEA process as specified in the CEA charter. The
29|CEA committee members will not have access to individual treatment codes for any patient or
30|clinical efficacy endpoint and safety event. The precise responsibilities and procedures applicable
31|for CEA will be detailed in the CEA charter.
