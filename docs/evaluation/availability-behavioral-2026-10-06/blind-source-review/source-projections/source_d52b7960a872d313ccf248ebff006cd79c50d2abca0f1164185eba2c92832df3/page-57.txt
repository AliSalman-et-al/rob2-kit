14|Data monitoring committee (DMC)
15|An independent data monitoring committee (DMC) will be appointed and will report to the
16|Executive Committee. The DMC will be responsible for safeguarding the interests of the patients
17|in the outcome study by assessing the safety of the intervention during the study. The DMC will
18|have access to the individual treatment codes and be able to merge these with the collected study
19|data while the study is ongoing. A charter will be prepared to detail precise roles and
20|responsibilities and procedures of the DMC.
