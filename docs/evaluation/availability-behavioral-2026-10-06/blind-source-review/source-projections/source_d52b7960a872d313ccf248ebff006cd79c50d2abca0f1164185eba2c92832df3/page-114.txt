11|BacGground
12|Dapagliflozin is a potent, highly selective and orally active inhibitor of human renal SGLT2. A
13|detailed description of the chemistry, pharmacology, efficacy, and safety of dapagliflozin is
14|provided in the InvestigatorOs Brochure. Supporting the hypothesis that dapagliflozin may reduce
15|CV Death and HF events in HF patients, irrespective of diabetes status, are observations from the
16|overall dapagliflozin clinical development programme. Dapagliflozin lowers HbA1c with a low
17|risk of inducing hypoglycaemia. In addition, dapagliflozin treatment has also been shown to reduce
18|weight and systolic blood pressure, and to have favourable effect on increased blood uric acid,
19|albuminuria, and arterial elasticity, conditions which are associated with increased CV and renal
20|risk HShigiyama et al 2017I. Dapagliflozin is believed be nephroprotective through non-glycaemic
21|mechanisms HQanner et al 2016I.
22|The identified blood pressure lowering effects, may reduce the primary outcome in a study
23|population with high prevalence of hypertension, similarly, the observed effects on body weight,
24|may be beneficial to the large part of the study population with obesity. The findings from EMPA-
25|REG, with a similar SGLT2 inhibitor compound, suggests that kidney function is preserved, or
26|improved in this diabetic study population. Furthermore, HFpEF patients are characterized by
27|fluid retention and a change in cardiac metabolism favouring glucose as substrate, both of which
28|has been hypothesised to be positively impacted by SGLT2 inhibitor treatment. Moreover, arterial
29|stiffness, and abnormal ventriculo-arterial coupling, are common in patients with HFpEF, and may
30|be modified by SGLT2 inhibitor treatments.
31|The clinical studies in healthy subjects at high multiple doses also show that, due to the mechanism
32|of action, dapagliflozin does not induce hypoglycemia in nondiabetic subjectsR however,
33|pharmacodynamic effects on glucose, sodium, and urinary volume are observed. Therefore, the
34|changes in these diabetes-independent mechanisms and intrarenal physiology are expected to be
35|similar regardless of underlying disease.
36|This study is an international, multicentre, parallel-group, event-driven, randomised, double-blind,
37|placebo-controlled study in HFpEF patients, evaluating the effect of dapagliflozin 10 mg, given
38|once daily in addition to background regional standard of care therapy, including treatments to
39|control co-morbidities, in reducing the composite of CV death and heart failure events
40|Hhospitalisations for HF or urgent HF visitsI.
