31|8.4.2
32|Vital Signs
33|x
34|Pulse and BP will be measured twice at all applicable visits, and all measurements will
35|be recorded in the eCRF.
36|x
37|The measurements should be done before any blood sampling. The measurements will
38|be assessed in a sitting position with a completely automated device. Manual techniques
39|will be used only if an automated device is not available.
40|x
41|The measurements should be preceded by at least 5 minutes of rest for the subject in
42|a quiet setting without distractions (e.g., television, cell phones).
43|8.4.3
44|Electrocardiogram
45|A 12-lead ECG (standard ECG with a paper speed of 25-50 mm/second covering at least
46|6 sequential beats) will be recorded at baseline (Visit 1) after the patient has been lying down to
47|rest for at least 5 minutes, to confirm presence or absence of atrial fibrillation/flutter at enrolment.
48|t rhythm will be reported in the eCRF. The baseline ECG should be stored and be made available
49|upon request for adjudication purposes.
