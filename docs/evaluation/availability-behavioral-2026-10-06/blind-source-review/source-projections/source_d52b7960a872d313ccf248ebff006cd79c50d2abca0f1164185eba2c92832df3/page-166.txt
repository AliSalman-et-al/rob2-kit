6|An adverse event is the development of any untoward medical occurrence in a subject or clinical
7|study subject administered a medicinal product and which does not necessarily have a causal
8|relationship with this treatment. An AE can therefore be any unfavourable and unintended sign
9|He.g. an abnormal laboratory findingI, symptom Hfor example nausea, chest painI, or disease
10|temporally associated with the use of a medicinal product, whether or not considered related to the
11|medicinal product.
12|The term AE is used to include both serious and non-serious AEs and can include a deterioration
13|of a pre- existing medical occurrence. An AE may occur at any time, including run-in or washout
14|periods, even if no Study treatment has been administered.
