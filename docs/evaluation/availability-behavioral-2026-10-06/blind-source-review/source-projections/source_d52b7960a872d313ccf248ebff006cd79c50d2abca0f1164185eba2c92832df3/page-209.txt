10|4.1.3
11|Confirmatory testing procedure
12|A closed testing procedure including a pre-specified hierarchical ordering of the primary and
13|secondary endpoints will be utilised. The Type I error will be controlled at an overall two-
14|sided 5I level for multiplicity across primary and secondary endpoints and in consideration
15|of the planned interim analysis. With one interim analysis at 67I of events the two-sided
16|significance level in final analysisH YH will be 4.980I. Statistical significance will be assessed
17|in the pre-specified order of the endpoints as specified in section 1.1.1 and section 1.1.2 . If
18|the primary endpoint is significant at level YH then the first secondary endpointH recurrent HF
19|hospitalisations and CV deathH will be tested at level Y. If the first secondary endpoint is
20|significantH then the Y will be split between KCCQ total symptom score and NYHA class. If
21|one of them is significant at level Y/2H then the other can be tested at level Y. If both KCCQ
22|and NYHA class reach statistical significanceH then all-cause mortality will be tested at
23|significance level Y.
24|If the study is stopped in the efficacy interim analysis (section 5)H testing of secondary
25|endpoints will be performed with the same testing procedure as described in this section above
26|with a two-sided YK 0.002.
27|4.1.4
28|Presentation of time-to-event analyses
29|In generalH summary tables of time-to-event analyses will include the number and percent of
30|patients with event per treatment groupH event rateH haDard ratio with 95I confidence interval
31|and p-value. The event rate will be derived as the number of patients with event divided by the
32|total duration of follow-up across all patients in the given group.
33|Kaplan-Meier (KM) estimates of the cumulative proportion of patients with events will be
34|calculated and plotted per treatment groupH with the number of patients at risk indicated below
35|the plot at specific time points. The KM plots will be presented for all time to event analysesH
36|including the individual components of the composite endpoints.
