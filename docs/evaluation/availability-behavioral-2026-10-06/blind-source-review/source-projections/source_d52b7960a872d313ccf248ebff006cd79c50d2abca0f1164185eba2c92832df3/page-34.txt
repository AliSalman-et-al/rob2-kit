18|Heart failure background standard of care
19|The patients should be on background standard of care therapies for patients with HFpEF
20|according to local guidelines, including diuretics when needed to control symptoms and volume

22|disease.
23|6.5.4
24|Anti-diabetes treatment
25|6.5.4.1
26|Background
27|More than 40% of patients with established HF are estimated to have T2D (Kristensen et al 2016).
28|Therefore, it is expected that a large proportion of patients will have an established T2D diagnosis
29|when included in this study and that some patients will develop T2D during the course of the study.
30|Treatment of diabetes should follow established guidelines, such as according to glycaemic goals
31|as recommended by the American Diabetes Association (ADA) and European Association for the
32|Study of Diabetes (EASD) in their joint Position Statement (Inzucchi et al 2012, Inzucchi et al
33|2015).
34|6.5.4.2
35|Treatment of patients with established diagnosis of type 2 diabetes
36|Diabetes medications at baseline and during the study will be recorded in the eCRF. Patients with
37|T2D at randomisation will continue their T2D treatment. SGLT2-inhibitors should be avoided (see
38|Section 6.5.1). Patients treated with insulin or insulin secretagogues have a higher risk of
39|experiencing hypoglycaemic events compared with those treated with other antidiabetic agents. If
40|needed, T2D treatments may be adjusted at the discretion of the Investigator or diabetes health
41|care provider.

49|Treatment after the end of the study
50|The patients will stop taking IP at the study closure visit (SCV). Remaining IP will be collected
