7|Concomitant therapy
8|All patients should be treated according to regional standard of care of HFpEF and existing
9|comorbidities Hincluding treatment of hypertension, ischemic heart disease, atrial fibrillation,
10|diabetes, hyperlipidaemiaI. Background medications should be part of clinical practice and will
11|not be provided by the Sponsor.
12|6.5.1
13|Prohibited medication
14|Concomitant treatment Hi.e., treatment in combination with IPI with open label SGLT2 inhibitors
15|e.g., dapagliflozin, empagliflozin, canagliflozin, ertugliflozin, tofogliflozin and luseogliflozin and
16|fix dose combinations containing these drugs should not be used. Also in situations when the
17|patient is not on IP, treatment with open label SGLT2 inhibitors during the study could interfere
18|with the interpretation of study results. If treatment with an SGLT2 inhibitor alone or in
19|combination is deemed essential, IP must be discontinued before that treatment is started.

24|mellitus.
25|6.5.3
26|Heart failure bacGground standard of care
27|The patients should be on background standard of care therapies for patients with HFpEF according
28|to local guidelines, including diuretics when needed to control symptoms and volume overload and
29|adequate treatment of co-morbidities such as hypertension and ischaemic heart disease.
30|6.5.4
31|Anti-diabetes treatment
32|6.5.4.1
33|BacGground
34|More than 40K of patients with established HF are estimated to have T2D HCristensen et al 2016I.
35|Therefore, it is expected that a large proportion of patients will have an established T2D diagnosis
36|when included in this study and that some patients will develop T2D during the course of the study.
37|Treatment of diabetes should follow established guidelines, such as according to glycaemic goals
38|as recommended by the American Diabetes Association HADAI and European Association for the
39|Study of Diabetes HEASDI in their joint Position Statement HInzucchi et al 2012, Inzucchi et al
40|2015I.
41|6.5.4.2
42|Treatment of patients with established diagnosis of type 2 diabetes
43|Diabetes medications at baseline and during the study will be recorded in the eCRF. Patients with
44|T2D at randomisation will continue their T2D treatment. SGLT2-inhibitors should be avoided Hsee
45|Section 6.5.1I. Patients treated with insulin or insulin secretagogues have a higher risk of
46|experiencing hypoglycaemic events compared with those treated with other antidiabetic agents. If
47|needed, T2D treatments may be adjusted at the discretion of the Investigator or diabetes health care
48|provider.
