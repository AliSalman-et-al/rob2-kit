11|7.1.1.3
12|Patients at risk of volume depletion
13|Temporary interruption of IP may be considered in patients thought to be at risk of volume
14|depletion/hypotension, such as patients with an acute medical illness potentially causing volume
15|depletion because of inadequate fluid intake or fluid/blood loss (e.g. gastroenteritis,
16|gastrointestinal haemorrhage), or those undergoing major surgery.
17|7.1.2
18|Procedures for discontinuation of study treatment
19| Investigators should instruct their patients to contact the site before or at the time IP is stopped.
20|A patient that decides to discontinue IP will always be asked about the reason(s) and the presence
21|of any AEs. Generally, AEs, SAEs, and potential endpoint events should not lead to IP
22|discontinuation, unless there is a clear clinical rationale to do so.
23|The date of last intake of IP should be documented in the eCRF. All IP should be returned by the
24|patient at their next on-site study visit or unscheduled visit. Patients permanently discontinuing
25|IP should be given locally available standard of care therapy, at the discretion of the Investigator.

37|7.2
38|Lost to follow-up
39|A patient will be considered potentially lost to follow-up if he or she fails to return for scheduled
40|visits and it is not possible for the site to get contact with the patient. To optimise the chance of
41|getting in contact with the patient during the study, Investigators should record as much contact
42|information as possible at the start of the study including home phone, mobile phone, holiday home
43|phone, family member phone numbers, email address, and social media contact details.
44|The following actions must be taken if a patient fails to return to the clinic for a required study
45|visit:
46|x The site must attempt to contact the patient and reschedule the missed visit as soon as possible
47|and counsel the patient on the importance of maintaining the assigned visit schedule.
