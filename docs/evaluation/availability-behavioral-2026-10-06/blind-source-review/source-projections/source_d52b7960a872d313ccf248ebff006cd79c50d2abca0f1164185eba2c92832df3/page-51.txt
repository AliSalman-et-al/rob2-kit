28|8.6.1.1
29|Reporting of SAEs considered to be potential endpoints
30|In order to avoid unnecessary unblinding of efficacy endpoint events, certain SAEs which are also
31|potential endpoints (i.e., fatal AEs and HF events) will not be reported to health authorities.
32|Clinical data for the above mentioned events will be recorded as AEs/SAEs as well as on separate
33|event forms in the eCRF. Recording of a suspected endpoint should be done within the same
34|timeframes as defined for SAEs (see Section 8.6.1).
35|In addition, fatal AEs and potential HF endpoints will be centrally adjudicated by an independent
36|CEA committee (see Section 8.3.1 and 8.3.2). If adjudication confirms the endpoint, the SAE will
37|not be reported to health authorities. However, if it is determined by the CEA committee that
38|a potential endpoint does not meet the endpoint criteria, the event will be reported (according to
39|the timelines specified in Section 8.6.1) to AZ patient safety data entry site and if applicable to the
40|health authorities (note that the clock starts when the adjudication results are available).
