8|A 3
9|Informed consent process
10|The Investigator or hisPher representative will explain the nature of the study to the subject or
11|hisPher legally authorised representative and answer all questions regarding the study.
12|Subjects must be informed that their participation is voluntary. Subjects or their legally authorised
13|representative will be required to sign a statement of informed consent that meets the requirements
14|of 21 CFR 50, local regulations, ICH guidelines, Health Insurance Portability and Accountability
15|Act HHIPAAI requirements, where applicable, and the IRBPIEC or study centre.
16|The medical record must include a statement that written informed consent was obtained before
17|the subject was enrolled in the study and the date the written consent was obtained. The authorised
18|person obtaining the informed consent must also sign the ICF.
19|Subjects must be re-consented to the most current version of the ICFHsI during their participation
20|in the study.
21|A copy of the ICFHsI must be provided to the subject or the subjectOs legally authorised
22|representative.
23|A subject who is rescreened is required to sign another ICF.
24|A 4
25|Data protection
26|Each subject will be assigned a unique identifier by the sponsor. Any subject records or data sets
27|transferred to the sponsor will contain only the identifierR subject names or any information which
28|would make the subject identifiable will not be transferred.
29|The subject must be informed that hisPher personal study- related data will be used by the sponsor
30|in accordance with local data protection law. The level of disclosure must also be explained to the
31|subject.
32|The subject must be informed that hisPher medical records may be examined by Clinical Muality
33|Assurance auditors or other authorised personnel appointed by the sponsor, by appropriate
34|IRBPIEC members, and by inspectors from regulatory authorities.
