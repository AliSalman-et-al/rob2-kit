21|Change from baseline at 8 months in the KCCQ total symptom score
22|The efficacy variable is the change from baseline at 8 months of the Kansas City
23|Cardiomyopathy Questionnaire (KCCQ) total symptom score (TSS).
24|The KCCQ is a self-administered disease specific instrument for patients with HF (Green et al
25|2000H Spertus et al 2005). The KCCQ consists of 23 items measuring HF-related symptomsH
26|physical limitationsH social limitationsH self-efficacyH and health-related Cuality of life. The
27|TSS incorporates the symptom burden and symptom freCuency domains into a single score.
28|Scores are transformed to a range of 0-100. Higher scores represent better outcomes.
29|Baseline is defined as the value at randomisation visit (visit 2). Change from baseline at each
30|post-baseline analysis time point will be calculated as the value at the corresponding post-
31|baseline analysis time point minus the baseline value. The KCCQ is assessed by the patient at
32|randomisationH at the visits targeted 1H 4 and 8 months following randomisation and at
33|premature treatment discontinuation visit (PTDV) and SCV. By the ITT principleH the analysis
34|will include all data irrespective of whether the patient has discontinued study drug.
