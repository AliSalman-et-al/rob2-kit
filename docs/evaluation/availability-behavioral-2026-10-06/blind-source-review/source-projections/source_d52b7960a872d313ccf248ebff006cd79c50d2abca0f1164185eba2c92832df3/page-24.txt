9|STUDY DESIGN
10|4.1
11|Overall design
12|This is an international, multicentre, parallel-group, event-driven, randomised, double-blind,
13|placebo-controlled study in HFpEF patients, evaluating the effect of dapagliflozin 10 mg versus
14|placebo, given once daily in addition to background regional standard of care therapy, including
15|treatments to control co-morbidities, in reducing the composite of CV death or heart failure events.
16|For an overview of the study design see Figure 1, Section 1.3. For details on treatments given
17|during the study, see Section 6.1.
18|For details on what is included in the efficacy and safety endpoints, see Section 3 Objectives and
19|Endpoints.

21|of structural heart disease) aged 40 \eaUV and ZiWh NYHA claVV II-IV who meet the inclusion
22|criteria, and none of the exclusion criteria, will be randomised in a 1:1 ratio to receive either
23|dapagliflozin 10 mg or placebo. Randomised treatment should be started as soon as possible and
24|within 24 hours after randomisation. It is estimated that approximately 8000 patients at
25|approximately 400-500 sites in 20-25 countries will be enrolled to reach the target of
26|approximately 4700 randomised patients.
27|Study closure procedures will be initiated when the predetermined number of primary endpoints
28|are predicted to have occurred (n=844), i.e. the Primary Analysis Censoring Date (PACD). Patients
29|should be scheduled for a Study Closure Visit (SCV) within 6 weeks of the PACD. The anticipated
30|total study duration is approximately 33 months dependent on randomisation rate and event rate.
31|The study duration, and the number of patients, may be changed if the randomisation rate or the
32|event rate is different than anticipated. The study may be terminated early if a clear harmful effect
33|of the study treatment is detected during the DMC review, or due to DMC recommendations
34|following pre-specified interim analyses (see Section 9.5).
35|Data on baseline characteristics, endpoints and AEs will be collected through a validated electronic
36|data capture (EDC) system with electronic case report forms (eCRFs).
