8|2.3.2
9|Potential benefits to patients
10|All patients in the study are expected to be optimally treated according to regional standard of care
11|therapy, including treatments to control co-morbidities, and dapagliflozin or placebo will be
12|administered on top of this treatment.
13|All patients participating in clinical trials irrespective of whether treated with active treatment or
14|not, generally receive closer medical attention than those in ordinary clinical practice which may
15|be to their advantage.
16|2.3.3
17|Conclusion
18|Considering the non-clinical and clinical experience with dapagliflozin and the precautions
19|included in the study protocol, participation in this study should present a minimal and thus
20|acceptable risk to eligible patients. Although hypothesis-generating data suggest beneficial effects
21|of SGLT2 inhibitors in patients with T2D with heart failure, at the time of writing of this clinical
22|study protocol, no available SGLT2 inhibitor has a treatment indication for patients with HFpEF.
23|The proposed clinical study will test the hypothesis that dapagliflozin reduces the risk of CV death
24|and HF events in patients with HFpEF, with or without T2D, in a rigorous fashion. The results
25|could potentially offer substantial benefit to patients with HFpEF, a patient population with a large
26|medical need for effective treatments.
