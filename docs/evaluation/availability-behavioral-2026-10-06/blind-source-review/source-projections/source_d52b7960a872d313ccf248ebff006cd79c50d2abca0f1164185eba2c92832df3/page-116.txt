13|Conclusion
14|Considering the non-clinical and clinical experience with dapagliflozin and the precautions
15|included in the study protocol, participation in this study should present a minimal and thus
16|acceptable risk to eligible patients. Although hypothesis-generating data suggest beneficial effects
17|of SGLT2 inhibitors in patients with T2D with heart failure, at the time of writing of this clinical
18|study protocol, no available SGLT2 inhibitor has a treatment indication for patients with HFpEF.
19|The proposed clinical study will test the hypothesis that dapagliflozin reduces the risk of CV death
20|and HF events in patients with HFpEF, with or without T2D, in a rigorous fashion. The results
21|could potentially offer substantial benefit to patients with HFpEF, a patient population with a large
22|medical need for effective treatments.
