8|Ethical and regulatory requirements
9|The principles for ethical and regulatory requirements for the study, including this genetics
10|research component, are outlined in Appendix A.
11|Informed consent
12|The genetic component of this study is optional and the subject may participate in other
13|components of the main study without participating in the genetic component. To participate in
14|the genetic component of the study the subject must sign and date both the consent form for the
15|main study and the genetic component of the study. Copies of both signed and dated consent forms
16|must be given to the subject and the original filed at the study centre. The Principal Investigator(s)
17|is responsible for ensuring that consent is given freely and that the subject understands that they
18|may freely withdrawal from the genetic aspect of the study at any time.
19|Subject data protection

21|employer, their family members, general physician unless required to do so by law.

29|Data management
30|Any genotype data generated in this study will be stored at a secure system at AstraZeneca and/or
31|designated organizations to analyse the samples.
32|AstraZeneca and its designated organisations may share summary results (such as genetic
33|differences from groups of individuals with a disease) from this genetic research with other
34|researchers, such as hospitals, academic organisations or health insurance companies. This can be
35|done by placing the results in scientific databases, where they can be combined with the results of
36|similar studies to learn even more about health and disease. The researchers can only use this
37|information for health-related research purposes. Researchers may see summary results but they
38|will not be able to see individual subject data or any personal identifiers.
39|Some or all of the clinical datasets from the main study may be merged with the genetic data in
40|a suitable secure environment separate from the clinical database.
