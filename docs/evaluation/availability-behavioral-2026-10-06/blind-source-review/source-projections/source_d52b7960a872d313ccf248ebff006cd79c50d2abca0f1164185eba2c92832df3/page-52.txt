10|8.6.2.1
11|Maternal exposure
12|Women of childbearing potential who are not using contraception as defined in Section 5.2;
13|exclusion criterion number 18 are not allowed to be included in this study. Should a pregnancy
14|still occur, the investigational product should be discontinued immediately and the pregnancy
15|reported to AstraZeneca.
16|Dapagliflozin must not be used in the second and third trimesters of pregnancy. In the time period
17|corresponding to second and third trimester of pregnancy with respect to human renal maturation,
18|maternal exposure to dapagliflozin in rat studies was associated with increased incidence and/or
19|severity of renal pelvic and tubular dilatations in progeny.
20|There are no adequate and well-controlled studies of dapagliflozin in pregnant women. When
21|pregnancy is detected, investigational product(s) should be discontinued.
22|8.6.3
23|Overdose
24|Dapagliflozin has been well tolerated at doses of up to 500 mg/day in single dose testing in healthy
25|volunteers and up to 100 mg/day in repeat dose testing for 14 days in healthy volunteers and
26|patients with T2D. Suspected single intake of more than 50 tablets of 10 mg dapagliflozin tablets
27|or repeated intake of more than 10 tablets of 10 mg dapagliflozin tablets should be reported on the
28|eCRF overdose module. If an overdose is suspected, monitoring of vital functions as well as
29|treatment should be performed as appropriate.
30|For further information regarding overdose, refer to the IB.
