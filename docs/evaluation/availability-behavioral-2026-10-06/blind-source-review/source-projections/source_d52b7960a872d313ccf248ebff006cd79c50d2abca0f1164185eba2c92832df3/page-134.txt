10|Lost to follow-up
11|A patient will be considered potentially lost to follow-up if he or she fails to return for scheduled
12|visits and it is not possible for the site to get contact with the patient. To optimise the chance of
13|getting in contact with the patient during the study, Investigators should record as much contact
14|information as possible at the start of the study including home phone, mobile phone, holiday home
15|phone, family member phone numbers, email address, and social media contact details.

17|visitD
