19|Missing KCCQ-TSS values at baseline or at 8 months will be imputed under the Missing at
20|Random assumption. The imputation will be done using a predictive mean matching multiple
21|imputation model and a method of Fully Conditional Specification as implemented in the SAS
22|Procedure MI (FCS statement). The predictive mean matching method ensures that the
23|imputed values remain in the permissible range of the KCCQ-TSS values. Imputation will be
24|done seHuentiallyO ieO imputing each time point in their chronological order and the
25|imputations at a given time point will be informed by preceding imputed time points. The
26|imputation model will include the treatment groupO T2D randomisation stratumO prior KCCQ-
27|TSS (at baselineO month 1 and month 4)O and three categorical variables representing the
28|number of HF events (categorised as 0O 1 or Q 2) in the intervals from randomisation to 1
29|monthO from 1 to 4 monthsO and from 4 to 8 monthsO respectivelyO depending on the time point
30|being imputed. Occurrences of HF events will be determined based on the investigator-
31|reported potential HF events. Auxiliary variables related to HF events are included in the
32|imputation model to improve the imputation accuracyO because the occurrence of HF events is
33|expected to be associated with HF symptoms as assessed by KCCQ-TSS.
34|The number of closest observations used to sample an imputed value by the predictive mean
35|matching method will be 5 (SAS default setting).
