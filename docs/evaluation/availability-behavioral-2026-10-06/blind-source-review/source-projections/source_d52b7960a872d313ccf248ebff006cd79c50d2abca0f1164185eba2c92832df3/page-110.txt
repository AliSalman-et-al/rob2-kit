39|Overall design:
40|This is an international, multicentre, parallel-group, event-driven, randomised, double- blind study
41|in patients with HFpEF, evaluating the effect of dapagliflozin 10 mg versus placebo, given once
42|daily in addition to background regional standard of care therapy, including treatments to control
43|co- morbidities, in reducing the composite of CV death and heart failure events Hhospitalisations
44|for HF or urgent HF visitsI. Adult patients aged U40 years with HFpEF HLVEF N40K and evidence
45|of structural heart diseaseI and New York Heart Association HNYHAI class II-IV who are eligible
46|according to the inclusionPexclusion criteria will be randomised in a 1D1 ratio to receive either
47|dapagliflozin 10 mg or placebo. Both out-patients and in-patients hospitalised for heart failure and
48|off intravenous heart failure-therapy for 24 hours can be randomised. It is estimated that
49|approximately 11000 patients at approximately 400-500 sites in 20-25 countries will need to be
50|enrolled to reach approximately 6100 randomised patients.
