33|6.4
34|Treatment compliance
35|The administration of all IP should be recorded in the appropriate sections of the eCRF. Any change
36|from the dosing schedule should be recorded in the eCRF.
37|Patients will be asked to return all unused IP and empty packages to the clinic at the site visit except
38|Visit 3. At each visit, any patient found to be non-compliant will be counselled on the importance
39|of taking their IP as prescribed. The investigator or delegate will enter the number of returned
40|tablets in the eCRF.
41|The Investigational Product Storage Manager is responsible for managing IP from receipt by the
42|study site until the destruction or return of all unused IP. The InvestigatorHsI is responsible for
43|ensuring that the patient has returned all unused IP.
