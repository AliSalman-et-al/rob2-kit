8|9.4.1
9|Efficacy analyses
10|9.4.1.1
11|Analysis of the primary variable
12|The primary variable is the time from randomisation to first event included in the primary
13|composite endpoint. The primary analysis will be based on the ITT principle using the FAS,
14|including events occurring on or prior to the PACD, adjudicated by the CEA committee.
15|In the analysis of the primary composite endpoint, treatments (dapagliflozin versus placebo) will
16|be compared using a Cox proportional hazards model with a factor for treatment group, stratified
17|by T2D status at randomisation. The p-value, HR and 95% confidence interval will be reported.
18|The contribution of each component of the primary composite endpoint to the overall treatment
19|effect will be examined. Methods similar to those described for the primary analysis will be used
20|to separately analyse the time from randomisation to the first occurrence of each component of the
21|primary composite endpoint. HR and 95% confidence intervals will be reported.
22|Kaplan-Meier estimates of the cumulative incidence to the first occurrence of any event in the
23|primary endpoint will be calculated and plotted, for overall analysis and for the individual
24|components.
25|9.4.1.2
26|Analysis of the secondary variables
27|The outcome of all HF hospitalisations (first and recurring) and CV death will be analysed by the
28|semi-parametric proportional rates model (Lin et al 2000) to test the treatment effect and to
29|quantify the treatment difference. The rate ratio and its 95% confidence interval and corresponding
30|two-sided p-value will be presented.
31|The proportion of patients with worsening NYHA classification from baseline to 8 months will be
32|analysed by a logistic regression with treatment group, baseline NYHA and T2D at randomisation
33|as factors. The odds ratio between treatment groups, its 95% confidence interval and corresponding
34|two-sided p-value will be presented.
35|The analysis of change from baseline for KCCQ total symptom score at 8 months will be further
36|detailed in the statistical analysis plan, e.g. with consideration of handling of patients who die. In
37|addition to the secondary endpoint, total symptom score, the overall summary score, clinical
38|summary score and domain scores will be analysed. A responder analysis will also be performed
39|(more details presented in the SAP).
40|The analysis of time from randomisation to all-cause mortality will be analysed in the similar
41|manner as the primary variable.
