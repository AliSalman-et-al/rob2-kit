11|National Lead Investigator (NLI) Committee
12|The National Lead Investigator (NLI) Committee will be comprised of NLIs from each country
13|where the study is conducted and supervised by the Executive Committee. Members of the
14|committee will be responsible for providing clinical guidance on study implementation,
15|recruitment and study conduct in their respective country.
16|
17|Data Monitoring Committee (DMC)
18|An independent DMC will be appointed and will report to the Executive Committee. The DMC
19|will be responsible for safeguarding the interests of the patients in the outcome study by assessing
20|the safety of the intervention during the study, and for reviewing the overall conduct of the study.
21|The DMC will have access to the individual treatment codes and be able to merge these with the
22|collected study data while the study is ongoing. A DMC charter will be prepared to detail precise
23|roles and responsibilities and procedures to ensure maintenance of the blinding and integrity of the
24|study in the review of accumulating data and interactions with the Executive Committee.
25|
26|Clinical Event Adjudication (CEA) Committee
27|The role of the CEA committee is to independently review, interpret and adjudicate potential
28|endpoints that are experienced by the patients. Endpoints will be identified preliminary by the
29|investigators, and also by AZ personnel or in the CEA process as specified in the CEA charter.
30|The CEA committee members will not have access to individual treatment codes for any patient
31|or clinical efficacy endpoint and safety event. The precise responsibilities and procedures
32|applicable for CEA will be detailed in the CEA charter.

36|A description of this clinical trial will be available on http://astrazenecaclinicaltrials.com and

38|available. The clinical trial and/or summary of main study results may also be available on other
39|websites according to the regulations of the countries in which the main study is conducted.

41|A 7
42|Data quality assurance
43|All subject data relating to the study will be recorded on printed or electronic CRF unless
44|transmitted to the sponsor or designee electronically (e.g. laboratory data). The Investigator is
45|responsible for verifying that data entries are accurate and correct by physically or electronically
46|signing the CRF.
47|The Investigator must maintain accurate documentation (source data) that supports the information
48|entered in the CRF.
