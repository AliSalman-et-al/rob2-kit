31|Statistical analyses
32|All personnel involved with the analysis of the study will remain blinded until database lock and
33|Clinical Study Protocol deviations identified.
34|Analyses will be performed by AstraZeneca or its representatives.
35|A comprehensive statistical analysis plan HSAPI will be developed prior to first patient randomised
36|and any subsequent amendments will be documented, with final amendments finalised before
37|database lock. This section is a summary of the planned statistical analyses of the primary and
38|secondary endpoints. Any deviations from this plan will be reported in the clinical study report.

40|Efficacy analyses
41|9.4.1.1
42|Analysis of the primary variable
43|The primary variable is the time from randomisation to first event included in the primary
44|composite endpoint. Two hypotheses will be tested simultaneously Hi.e., dual primary endpoint
45|analysesID H1I in the full population and H2I in an LVEF J60K subpopulation, with alpha allocated
46|to each test. The primary analysis will be based on the ITT principle using the FAS, including
47|events occurring on or prior to the PACD, adjudicated by the CEA committee.
48|In the analysis of the primary composite endpoint, treatments Hdapagliflozin versus placeboI will
49|be compared using a Cox proportional hazards model with a factor for treatment group, stratified
50|by T2D status at randomisation. The p-value, HR and 95K confidence interval will be reported.
