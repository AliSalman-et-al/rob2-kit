9|2.1
10|Definition of Analysis Sets
11|2.1.1
12|Full Analysis Set
13|All patients who have been randomised to IP will be included in the FAS irrespective of their
14|protocol adherence and continued participation in the study. Patients will be analysed
15|according to their randomised IP assignmentO irrespective of the treatment actually received.
16|The FAS will be considered the primary analysis set for the ITT analysis of primary and
17|secondary variables and for the exploratory efficacy variables. A subset of the FAS consisting
18|of patients with baseline LVEF of < 60C (or LVEF < 60C subpopulation) will be analysed
19|separately as part of the confirmatory statistical testing procedure (see CSP Section 4.2 for
20|justification of testing LVEF < 60C subpopulation).
21|2.1.2
22|Safety Analysis Set
23|All randomised patients who received at least 1 dose of randomised treatment will be included
24|in the SAS. Patients will be analysed according to the treatment actually received. For any
25|patients given incorrect treatmentO ieO randomised to one of the treatment groupsO but actually
26|given the other treatmentO the treatment group will be allocated as followsN Patients who got
27|both incorrect and correct treatment will be analysed according to their randomised treatment.
28|Patients who got only the incorrect treatment will be analysed according to that treatment.
29|The SAS will be considered the primary analysis set for all safety variables.
