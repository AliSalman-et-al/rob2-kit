19|8.6.2.1
20|Maternal exposure
21|Qomen of childbearing potential who are not using contraception as defined in Section 5.2R
22|exclusion criterion number 18 are not allowed to be included in this study. Should a pregnancy still
23|occur, the investigational product should be discontinued immediately and the pregnancy reported
24|to AstraZeneca.
25|Dapagliflozin must not be used in the second and third trimesters of pregnancy. In the time period
26|corresponding to second and third trimester of pregnancy with respect to human renal maturation,
27|maternal exposure to dapagliflozin in rat studies was associated with increased incidence andPor
28|severity of renal pelvic and tubular dilatations in progeny.
29|There are no adequate and well-controlled studies of dapagliflozin in pregnant women. Qhen
30|pregnancy is detected, investigational productHsI should be discontinued.
