29|Sample size determination
30|The primary objective of the study is to determine the superiority of dapagliflozin versus placebo
31|added to standard of care in reducing the composite of CV death and heart failure events
32|(hospitalisation for HF or urgent HF visit). Assuming a true hazard ratio (HR) of 0.80 between
33|dapagliflozin and placebo, using a two-sided alpha of 5%, 844 primary endpoint events will
34|provide a statistical power of 90% for the test of the primary composite endpoint. This is based
35|on an overall 1:1 allocation between dapagliflozin and placebo.
36|The HR was chosen as a conservative assumption based on the observed HR 0.72 (95% confidence
37|interval 0.50-1.04) for the composite of HF hospitalisation and CV death in patients with HF at
38|baseline in the EMPA-REG OUTCOME trial (Fitchett et al 2016) and HR 0.61 (0.46-0.80) for
39|patients with history of HF in the CANVAS program (Rndholm eW al 2018) considering that these
40|were post-hoc analyses in subgroups with limited documentation of baseline HF diagnosis, not
41|characterised by ejection fraction.
