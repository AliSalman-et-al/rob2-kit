32|The clinical studies in healthy subjects at high multiple doses also show that, due to the
33|mechanism of action, dapagliflozin does not induce hypoglycemia in nondiabetic subjects;
34|however, pharmacodynamic effects on glucose, sodium, and urinary volume are observed.
35|Therefore, the changes in these diabetes-independent mechanisms and intrarenal physiology are
36|expected to be similar regardless of underlying disease.
37|This study is an international, multicentre, parallel-group, event-driven, randomised, double-
38|blind, placebo-controlled study in HFpEF patients, evaluating the effect of dapagliflozin 10 mg,
39|given once daily in addition to background regional standard of care therapy, including treatments
40|to control co-morbidities, in reducing the composite of CV death and heart failure events
41|(hospitalisations for HF or urgent HF visits).
