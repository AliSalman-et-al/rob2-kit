7|Scientific rationale for study design
8|This is a randomised, multi-centre, double-blind, parallel-group study. Randomisation and double-
9|blinding will minimise potential bias. The target population includes adult Haged U 40 yearsI male
10|and female patients with HFpEF, which is defined in this study as individuals with an established
11|diagnosis of heart failure and a LVEF N40K and structural heart disease who meet natriuretic
12|peptide thresholds. The requirement of demonstrated structural heart disease Hi.e. left ventricular
13|hypertrophy or left atrial enlargement1I and elevated natriuretic peptides aims to support the
14|diagnosis of heart failure, since other common co-morbidities may cause overlapping symptoms.
15|Most randomised patients will be out-patients. However, to address a specific need in a period with
16|high risk for events, a proportion of patients will be enrolled and randomised during hospitalisation
17|for heart failure or within 30 days of discharge from hospitalisation for heart failure Hsubacute
18|subgroupI.
19|The study population will include patients both with and without T2D, as the beneficial
20|haemodynamic effects of dapagliflozin appear to be independent of the glycaemic effect, and can
21|therefore be expected in both groups. Enrolment in the study may be capped based on the
22|proportion of patients withPwithout T2D, in certain LVEF categories, in each NYHA class,
23|withPwithout atrial fibrillation, randomised during or early after HF hospitalisation Hsubacute
24|subgroupI, and geographic region.
25|The control group will receive placeboR there are no approved pharmacological treatments for
26|HFpEF that could be utilised as a comparator. All patients will be treated according to local
27|guidelines on standard of care treatment for patients with HFpEF, focusing on treatment of HF
28|symptoms He.g. diureticsI and comorbidities Hincluding treatment for high blood pressure,
29|ischaemic heart disease, atrial fibrillationI.
30|The study population will include patients with eGFR U 25 mlPminP1.73m2. Patients with reduced
31|renal function have a clinical picture with increased intra-glomerular pressure, hypertension,
32|proteinuria and fluidPsodium overload and SGLT2 inhibition can improve all these abnormalities
33|through metabolic-independent mechanisms. Thus, patients with heart failure and reduced renal
34|function could be expected to benefit from treatment with dapagliflozin.
35|The primary efficacy endpoints of the study are adjudicated CV death and HF events
36|Hhospitalisation for HF or urgent HF visitI. The rationale for selecting CV death over all-cause
37|death is the expectation that HF treatment will decrease CV death and not all potential causes of
38|death HZannad et al 2014I. Heart failure events include both HF hospitalisations and unplanned HF
39|visits requiring urgent treatment independently of whether the exacerbation of HF results in
40|hospitalisation Haccording to CDISC definitionsR Hicks et al 2014R Hicks et al 2018I. These are the
41|same endpoint definitions currently employed in the SponsorOs HFrEF outcome study HDapa-HFR
