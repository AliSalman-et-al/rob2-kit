24|In addition to the subgroups listed in the rSAP, the following subgroups of interest will be
25|explored to examine event rates and for consistency of efficacy and safety of dapagliflozin. All
26|subgroups will be identified based on randomization or pre-randomization data unless otherwise
27|specified. For each subgroup, we will assess the treatment effect and interaction with treatment
28|for the primary endpoint and each of the secondary endpoints, including components of the
29|primary endpoint, measures of quality of life (KCCQ), NYHA class, and the renal composite
30|endpoint. In addition, all subgroups will be assessed in the LVEF < 60% subgroup.
