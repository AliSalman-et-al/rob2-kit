7|1.2.2
8|Withdrawal of consent
9|Withdrawal of consent (WoC) should only occur if the patient has received appropriate
10|information about options for modified study follow-up and does not agree to any kind of
11|further assessment or follow-up. Information regarding vital status (dead or alive) at the end of
12|the study will be collected from public sourcesH to be included in the analysis of death from
13|any cause as a sole outcome and in patient disposition summaries.
14|1.2.3
15|Discontinuation from study drug
16|Discontinuation from study drug does not mean discontinuation from study follow-up or
17|WoC. Patients who discontinue from study drug should continue study visits according to plan
18|until study closure. If the patient does not agree to this approachH modified follow-up
19|capturing the essential information for the objectives of the study should be arranged. Data
20|will be included in the ITT analyses irrespective of whether the event occurred before or
21|following discontinuation of study drug.
22|1.2.4
23|Vital Status
24|Known vital status at the end of the study will be defined when the patient is dead or has date
25|last know alive on or after the PACD.
26|For patients who have withdrawn consentH the investigator will attempt to collect vital status
27|from publicly available sources at study closure in compliance with local privacy
28|laws/practices.
29|1.2.5
30|Lost to follow-up
31|The term lost to follow-up (LTFU) will be limited to patients with unknown vital status at the
32|end of the study as defined in section 1.2.4. Other measures will be used to describe
33|completeness of follow-up of the primary endpoint (section 4.1.5)
34|1.3
35|Study design
36|This is an internationalH multicentreH parallel-groupH event-drivenH randomisedH double-blindH
37|placebo-controlled study in patients with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction
38|(HFpEF)H evaluating the effect of dapaglifloDin 10 mg versus placeboH given once daily in
39|addition to background regional standard of care therapyH including treatments to control co-
40|morbiditiesH in reducing the composite of CV death or heart failure events.
41|HFpEF is defined for the purposes of this study as left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF)
42|F40I and evidence of structural heart disease. Adult patients with HFpEFH aged J40 years
43|and with NYHA class II-IV will be randomised in a 1G1 ratio to receive either dapaglifloDin
44|10 mg or placebo once daily. A proportion of patientsH here denoted as the subacute groupH
45|will be randomised during hospitalisation for heart failure or within 21 days of discharge from
46|hospitalisation for heart failure.
