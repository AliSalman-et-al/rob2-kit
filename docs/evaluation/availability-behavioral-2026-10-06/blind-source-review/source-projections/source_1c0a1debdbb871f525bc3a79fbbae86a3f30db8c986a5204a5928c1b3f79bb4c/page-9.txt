18|ment of symptom burden was limited to patients

21|results were similar in all patients for whom
22|data were available. Because all the subgroups in
23|the DELIVER trial were underpowered, within-
24|subgroup results should be interpreted cau-
25|tiously.
26|Among patients with heart failure and a
27|mildly reduced or preserved ejection fraction,
28|dapagliflozin resulted in a lower risk of the pri-
29|mary composite outcome (worsening heart fail-
30|ure or cardiovascular death), in fewer worsening
31|heart failure events and cardiovascular deaths,
32|and in a lower symptom burden, with no excess
33|of adverse events. Findings were consistent
34|across prespecified subgroups, including those
35|defined according to left ventricular ejection
36|fraction. These data provide further evidence to
37|support the use of an SGLT2 inhibitor as es-
38|sential therapy in patients with heart failure,
39|regardless of the presence or absence of type 2
40|diabetes mellitus or left ventricular ejection
41|fraction.
