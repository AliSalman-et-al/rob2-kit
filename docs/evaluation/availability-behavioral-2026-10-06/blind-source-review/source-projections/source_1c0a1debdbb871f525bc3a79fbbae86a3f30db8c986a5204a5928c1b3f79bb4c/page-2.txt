52|Methods
53|Trial Design and Oversight
54|The DELIVER trial was a phase 3, international,
55|multicenter, parallel-group, event-driven, double-
56|blind, randomized, controlled trial in which pa-
57|tients with chronic heart failure and a left ven-
58|tricular ejection fraction of more than 40%
59|received dapagliflozin or matching placebo, in
60|addition to their usual therapy. The steering com-
61|mittee designed and oversaw the conduct of the
62|trial and the analysis of the data in collaboration
63|with the sponsor (AstraZeneca). The trial proto-
64|col was approved by a local or central institu-
65|tional review board at each trial center. The au-
66|thors who had access to the data vouch for the
67|accuracy and completeness of the data, and all
68|the authors vouch for the fidelity of the trial to
69|the protocol. Details regarding the design of the
70|trial are provided in the protocol and in the Sup-
71|plementary Appendix, both of which are avail-
72|able with the full text of this article at NEJM.org.
73|Trial Patients
74|Patients were eligible for enrollment if they were
75|at least 40 years of age; had stabilized heart
76|failure, with or without type 2 diabetes mellitus;
77|had a left ventricular ejection fraction of more
78|than 40%; had evidence of structural heart dis-
79|ease; and had an elevated natriuretic peptide
80|level. Patients who had had a previous left ven-
81|tricular ejection fraction of 40% or less were
82|eligible provided that they had an ejection frac-
83|tion of more than 40% at the time of enroll-
84|ment. Patients could have been enrolled either as
85|outpatients or during hospitalization for heart
86|failure. Detailed inclusion and exclusion criteria
87|have been published previously9 and are provid-
88|ed in Table S1 in the Supplementary Appendix.
89|Trial Procedures and Outcomes
90|All the patients provided written informed con-
91|sent. Those who met the inclusion and exclusion
92|criteria were randomly assigned to receive dapa-
93|gliflozin at a dose of 10 mg once daily or match-
