19|BACKGROUND
20|Sodium-glucose cotransporter 2 (SGLT2) inhibitors reduce the risk of hospitaliza-
21|tion for heart failure and cardiovascular death among patients with chronic heart
22|failure and a left ventricular ejection fraction of 40% or less. Whether SGLT2 in-
23|hibitors are effective in patients with a higher left ventricular ejection fraction
24|remains less certain.
25|METHODS
26|We randomly assigned 6263 patients with heart failure and a left ventricular ejec-
27|tion fraction of more than 40% to receive dapagliflozin (at a dose of 10 mg once
28|daily) or matching placebo, in addition to usual therapy. The primary outcome was
29|a composite of worsening heart failure (which was defined as either an unplanned
30|hospitalization for heart failure or an urgent visit for heart failure) or cardiovas-
31|cular death, as assessed in a time-to-event analysis.
32|RESULTS
33|Over a median of 2.3 years, the primary outcome occurred in 512 of 3131 patients
34|(16.4%) in the dapagliflozin group and in 610 of 3132 patients (19.5%) in the pla-
35|cebo group (hazard ratio, 0.82; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.73 to 0.92; P<0.001).
36|Worsening heart failure occurred in 368 patients (11.8%) in the dapagliflozin
37|group and in 455 patients (14.5%) in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.79; 95%
38|CI, 0.69 to 0.91); cardiovascular death occurred in 231 patients (7.4%) and 261
39|patients (8.3%), respectively (hazard ratio, 0.88; 95% CI, 0.74 to 1.05). Total events
40|and symptom burden were lower in the dapagliflozin group than in the placebo
41|group. Results were similar among patients with a left ventricular ejection fraction
42|of 60% or more and those with a left ventricular ejection fraction of less than 60%,
43|and results were similar in prespecified subgroups, including patients with or
44|without diabetes. The incidence of adverse events was similar in the two groups.
45|CONCLUSIONS
46|Dapagliflozin reduced the combined risk of worsening heart failure or cardiovas-
47|cular death among patients with heart failure and a mildly reduced or preserved
48|ejection fraction. (Funded by AstraZeneca; DELIVER ClinicalTrials.gov number,
49|NCT03619213.)
