49: Results
50: Trial Patients
51: In all, 14,752 patients (of whom 10,782 [73.1%] 
52: had previous cardiovascular disease) underwent 
53: randomization from June 18, 2010, through Sep-
54: tember 16, 2015, and were included in the inten-
55: tion-to-treat population; 7356 patients were as-
56: signed to receive exenatide and 7396 to receive 
57: placebo once weekly (Fig. S2 in the Supplemen-
58: tary Appendix).22 The planned closeout of follow-
59: up of the patients was from December 5, 2016, 
60: to May 11, 2017, after the prespecified required 
61: minimum of 1360 patients were confirmed to 
62: have had a primary composite outcome event.
63: The demographic, disease, and clinical char-
64: acteristics of the patients did not differ signifi-
65: cantly between the groups, nor did the use of 
66: glucose-lowering agents or medications to reduce 
67: the risk of cardiovascular disease, with the excep-
68: tion of lipid-lowering medications and SGLT-2 
69: inhibitors (Table S1 in the Supplementary Ap-
70: pendix).22 At baseline, the median length of time 
71: that patients had had diabetes was 12.0 years 
72: (interquartile range, 7.0 to 18.0), the median 
73: glycated hemoglobin level was 8.0% (interquar-
74: tile range, 7.3 to 8.9) (63.9 mmol per mole [inter-
75: quartile range, 56.3 to 73.8]), and 2389 patients 
76: (16.2%) had a history of heart failure.
77: A total of 14,187 patients (96.2%) completed 
78: the trial, and vital status was obtained for 98.8% 
79: of the patients. The median duration of follow-
80: up was 3.2 years (interquartile range, 2.2 to 4.4; 
81: maximum, 6.8) and was similar in the two 
82: groups. The median duration of exposure to the 
83: trial regimen was 2.4 years (interquartile range, 
84: 1.4 to 3.8) in the exenatide group and 2.3 years 
85: (interquartile range, 1.2 to 3.6) in the placebo 
86: group. The mean percentage of time that partici-
87: pants received the trial regimen (i.e., the dura-
88: tion of time that participants received the trial 
89: regimen relative to the duration of time that they 
90: were expected to receive the regimen during the 
91: trial) was 76.0% and 75.0%, respectively. Prema-
92: ture discontinuation of the trial regimen was 
93: primarily the result of decision by the patient 
94: (Table S2 in the Supplementary Appendix).
