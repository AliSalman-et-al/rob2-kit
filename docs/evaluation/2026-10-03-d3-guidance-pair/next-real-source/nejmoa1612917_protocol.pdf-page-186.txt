33: Control of Type I Error
34: Type I error will be controlled at a one-sided 0.025 level for multiplicity across primary and 
35: secondary objectives and in consideration of planned interim analyses. The alpha of 0.02495 
36: represents the final one-sided significance level to be used when the study has been completed 
37: in entirety. At an interim analysis, testing for superiority of primary efficacy endpoint will 
38: occur, and the significance level for superiority will be replaced by one-sided 0.00005 at the 
39: first and one-sided 0.0005 at the second interim (see Section 4.7). Statistical testing will 
40: proceed sequentially and statistical significance will be assessed in the following hierarchical
41: order. When a test is found to be statistically significant, testing will proceed to the next test. 
42: If a test is not statistically significant, the subsequent one will not be assessed for statistical 
43: significance although nominal p-values will be provided.
