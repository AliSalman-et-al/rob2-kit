5: Lost to Follow-Up
6: All reasonable efforts must be made to locate patients to determine and report their ongoing 
7: status. This includes follow-up with persons authorized by the patient as noted above as well as 
8: the usual care provider. Lost to follow-up is defined by the inability to reach the patient after a 
9: minimum of three documented phone calls, as well as lack of response by patient to one 
10: registered mail letter. All attempts should be documented in the patient’s medical records. If it is 
11: determined that the patient has died, the site will use permissible local methods to obtain the date 
12: and cause of death.
13: If an investigator’s use of a third-party representative to assist in the follow-up portion of the 
14: study has been included in the patient’s informed consent, then the investigator may use a 
15: Sponsor-retained third-party representative to assist site staff with obtaining patient’s contact 
16: information or other public vital status data necessary to complete the follow-up portion of the 
17: study. Where specific consent has been obtained from the participant, the site staff and 
18: representative will consult publicly available sources, such as public health registries and 
19: databases, in order to obtain updated contact information. If after all attempts, the patient
20: remains lost to follow-up, then the last known alive date as determined by the investigator should 
21: be reported and documented in the patient’s medical records.
22: 6.17
23: Breaking the Blind
24: The IVRS will be used to unblind patients to the randomized treatment assignment only if 
25: absolutely necessary. Disclosure envelopes will not be supplied with the clinical supplies. Drug 
26: identification information is to be unmasked ONLY if necessary for the welfare of the patient. 
27: Prior to unblinding, the investigator is required to speak with a trial hotline physician (Section 
28: 6.18).  Patients whose treatment has been unblinded can continue to receive trial medication and 
29: can continue to be followed in the trial as described in this protocol.
30: 6.18
31: Trial Hotline
32: Clinicians at DCRI and DTU operate the trial hotline. It is available at all times to answer urgent 
33: clinical questions from sites concerning enrolled patients as well as questions to determine 
34: whether a particular patient qualifies for enrollment or performance of study procedures.
