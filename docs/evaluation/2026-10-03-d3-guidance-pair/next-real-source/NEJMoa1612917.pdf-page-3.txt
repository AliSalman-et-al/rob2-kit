31: Outcomes
32: The primary outcome was defined as the first 
33: occurrence of any component of the composite 
34: outcome of death from cardiovascular causes, 
35: nonfatal myocardial infarction, or nonfatal stroke 
36: (three-component MACE outcome), in a time-to-
37: event analysis. Secondary outcomes included 
38: death from any cause, death from cardiovascular 
39: causes, and the first occurrence of nonfatal or 
40: fatal myocardial infarction, nonfatal or fatal 
41: stroke, hospitalization for acute coronary syn-
42: drome, and hospitalization for heart failure, in 
43: time-to-event analyses. An independent clinical 
44: events classification committee whose members 
45: were unaware of the trial-group assignments ad-
46: judicated all the components of the primary com-
47: posite outcome, secondary outcomes, ventricular 
48: arrhythmias that led to intervention, neoplasms, 
49: and pancreatitis; these events are defined in the 
50: Clinical Event Definitions section in the Supple-
51: mentary Appendix.

80: Statistical Analysis
81: Information on sample size and power calcula-
82: tions has been published previously 21; we esti-
83: mated that with 1360 patients with a confirmed 
84: primary composite outcome event, the trial would 
85: have 85% power to detect a risk of a primary 
86: composite outcome event that was 15% lower 
87: with once-weekly administration of exenatide 
88: than with placebo, at a two-sided alpha level of 
89: 0.05. The time-to-event analyses were performed 
90: with the use of a Cox proportional-hazards 
91: model for primary, secondary, and exploratory 
92: outcomes in the intention-to-treat population, 
93: stratified according to history of cardiovascular 
94: disease, with trial regimen as an explanatory 
95: variable. The intention-to-treat population includ-
96: ed all patients who underwent randomization.
