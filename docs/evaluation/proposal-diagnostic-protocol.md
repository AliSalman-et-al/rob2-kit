# Proposal diagnostic invocation and correction rules

One authorized invocation means exactly one fresh model process. No operator relaunch, second invocation, resumed session, operator-written candidate, or manual draft repair is permitted. Within that invocation the model may inspect feedback and self-correct its own complete proposal up to the specified validation-attempt guard (four where authorized). Stop after successful validation and scope-review inspection, or the configured guard/failure. This does not authorize saving, researcher approval, domains, another case, or a full benchmark.

Future diagnostic wording: “You may self-correct construction or scientific support within this same invocation, up to four validate_proposal calls. The prohibition on retries applies to operator relaunches or additional invocations, not these permitted in-invocation corrections. No operator may fill or repair your draft.”
