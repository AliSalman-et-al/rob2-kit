# Independent source-grounded review criteria

Source review criteria, frozen before any inference:

Primary outcome is first-pass usable, source-grounded submission with citations
resolved to a common representation and complete active answers. Classify
transport failure, citation attribution failure, unsupported inference and
defensible judgment differences separately. Blind semantic review to interface
when feasible. Repair count, actions, time and tokens are secondary. One exposed
case cannot establish a general performance advantage.

- Every asserted count/reason belongs to the stated arm, analysis population,
  time window and endpoint; distinguish original exenatide non-dosing and later
  placebo crossover from the selected week-26 contrast.
- Study completion, treated/ITT membership, primary-analysis denominator,
  directly observed endpoint and LOCF-filled value are distinguished. No invented
  rescue/discontinuation overlap or endpoint denominator.
- ANCOVA LOCF is the frozen reported estimator; acknowledge the actual MMRM
  evidence without automatically calling it either a cure or a demonstrated
  sensitivity analysis for this contrast.
- Cochrane warrants address magnitude/context, evidence against bias, possible
  versus likely dependence, relevant outcome-related rescue and real uncertainty.
  No preferred answer vector/domain label, universal 95% gate or mandatory MNAR
  test. Independent reviewers can accept more than one defensible judgment.
- Official full guidance §6.1 p. 39 assigns excluded **available** outcomes to
  D2; genuinely missing/imputed outcomes belong to D3. Rescue hyperglycemia is
  contextual evidence, not proof that omitted measurements were unobserved.
  Do not count pre-rescue exclusions as missing observed HbA1c or force 3.4.
  Official 3.2 has no NI choice. Reached 3.4 N/PN leads to Some concerns;
  Y/PY/NI leads to High. Neither branch is a preferred answer for this case.
- Full source/counterpoint/uncertainty and Result binding survive native saving;
  canonical acceptance alone is not scientific correctness.

Objectively wrong shortcuts include treating 260/124 completers or 271/119 analyzed
as observed week-26 counts; 19/17 discontinuations or derived 8/22 as known missing
HbA1c; equating placebo 22 analysis exclusions with 22 rescues; summing rescue and
dropout without overlap information; importing other arms, 52-week counts or
post-week-26 rules; substituting MMRM for ANCOVA LOCF; treating safety follow-up
as guaranteed HbA1c measurement; treating LOCF/MMRM presence as proof of absence
of bias or absence of a named MNAR method as proof of bias; asserting no SAP ever
existed; or claiming bias direction without support. Qualified inferences and
explicitly derived counts remain legitimate.


Authority: [Cochrane RoB 2 guidance, 22 August 2019](https://www.cochrane.de/sites/cochrane.de/files/uploads/RoB_2.0_guidance_2019.pdf).
