## What this result establishes and limits

The result does not support a claim that the contrastive sentence improves these
repairs or is necessary to obtain them: the ordinary arm already achieves all
primary repairs. Adoption of the contrastive prompt is not justified by this
pair. It also provides no benchmark agreement, general accuracy comparison,
Domain risk-label validation or benefit evidence for the separate snapshot
information-preservation repair. Two stochastic outputs and four synthetic
worlds cannot establish equivalence of the prompts or rule out benefit in a
harder/different setting. Thirteen warrants are nested, not 13 independent trials.

## Next scientific mechanism

The remaining concrete signal is **unaccounted selected-outcome status becoming
certain missingness inside an otherwise source-grounded warrant**. Both finding
explanations show this substitution, and the contrastive draft retains it. A
useful next audit should locate that same state/qualification loss in preserved
native D2/D3 assessment traces, then determine whether it changes dependent
reasoning or only wording. Track selected-endpoint observation separately from
attendance, analysis membership, unrelated missing variables and uncertainty
about ascertainment; preserve defensible probable inference without imposing an
exact-count or NI gate.

This is a targeted audit direction, not a demonstrated fix. Establish a natural
failure and source/result alignment before another experiment or architecture
change. If a concrete repair follows, evaluate it on the requested Code benchmark
repository's cases with their exact selected result and captured sources, retain
reference/source ambiguities and inspect disagreements independently. Do not
substitute this synthetic panel for those cases, construct another generic
reviewer merely because it sounds useful, or infer model benefit from a typed
information-preservation change alone. No new paid run, full benchmark,
production-default change or contrastive-prompt adoption follows from this
comparison.
