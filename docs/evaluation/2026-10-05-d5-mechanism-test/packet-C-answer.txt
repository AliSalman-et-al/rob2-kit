**5.1 — Probably yes**

**Firm evidence:** A detailed analysis plan exists within the protocol, independently of the later SAP. The original protocol is dated **12 February 2016**, and the final protocol **3 November 2016** (Protocol/SAP p. 2, lines 6–14). Both precede recruitment, reported as January–August 2017 (Article p. 3, lines 79–82).

The original protocol specifies:

- Oral semaglutide versus placebo, all randomized participants, hazard ratio, and inclusion of events regardless of treatment adherence (Protocol/SAP p. 62, lines 35–46; p. 63, lines 15–16).
- FAS analyzed as randomized; observation from randomization through final contact, scheduled five weeks after the last planned dose; censoring and adjudicated event dates (p. 64, lines 19–48).
- First three-component MACE, analyzed using a stratified Cox model with treatment as a fixed factor (p. 64, lines 49–56).

These specifications are retained in the final protocol (p. 175, lines 35–46; p. 176, lines 15–16; p. 177, lines 19–56). The final SAP explicitly specifies the treatment-policy FAS, in-trial stratified Cox HR and two-sided 95% CI (p. 206, lines 35–46). The reported result matches these central specifications: all randomized participants, in-trial follow-up, stratified Cox analysis, and HR 0.79 (Article p. 3, lines 58–69; p. 5, lines 4–8; p. 7, lines 120–123).

**Reasonable inference:** The numerical result probably followed intentions established before investigators could access unblinded outcomes. The protocol required maintained blinding, prohibited unblinded between-group analyses before lock except confidential DMC-related analyses, and required SAP finalization before lock (Protocol/SAP p. 175, lines 15–23). Primary-endpoint safety reports were also to remain blinded for the sponsor (p. 166, lines 15–28).

**Unresolved:** The SAP is marked final on **1 November 2018** (p. 198, lines 3–11), after the registry’s actual completion date of **25 September 2018** (Registry p. 1, lines 1661–1662, 1672–1673). Actual database-lock, release and investigator-unblinding dates are unavailable. Completion does not establish unblinding. Consequently, the timing of final refinements—including the post-follow-up death rule at p. 205, lines 64–69—cannot be definitively verified. This supports **probably_yes**, rather than an unqualified yes.

**5.3 — Probably no**

**Firm evidence:** The SAP distinguishes the primary analysis from an additionally adjusted analysis and two on-treatment analyses using 38-day and 7-day windows (Protocol/SAP p. 207, lines 25–40; p. 208, lines 15–20). The appendix identifies those three sensitivity analyses, and the article reports their consistency with the primary result (Appendix p. 19, lines 3–16; Article p. 5, lines 8–10). The supplied text does not expose their numerical figure values, limiting verification.

Additional tipping-point and out-of-trial analyses were conditional on establishing superiority (Protocol/SAP p. 208, lines 21–38). Superiority was not established, with P=0.17 (Article p. 5, lines 6–8), so their absence is explained by the planned condition.

**Reasonable inference:** The early specified primary model, explicit sensitivity-analysis roles and evidence of their reporting argue against results-driven selection among analyses.

**Unresolved:** Complete numerical reporting and final-SAP chronology remain uncertain. The superiority-hierarchy amendment was attributed to findings from **SUSTAIN 6**, rather than observed PIONEER 6 results (Protocol/SAP p. 212, lines 21–27); that amendment alone does not establish selective reporting.

The **Low** judgment rests on the detailed pre-recruitment specification of the target and reasonable evidence against selection across measurements or analyses. It remains a supported judgment with uncertainty: the supplied evidence neither proves every final refinement preceded unblinding nor establishes a results-driven choice warranting High risk.
