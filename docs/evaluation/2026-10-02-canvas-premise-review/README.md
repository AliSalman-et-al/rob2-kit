### Eligibility and the synthetic measurement control

The integrated plan lists primary and supportive analyses separately (protocol pp.613 / 672), including missing-data imputation, on-treatment analysis, components and subgroups. Protocol p.649 adds first-30/60/90-day summaries and individual components. The report's Figure 2 (main p.8) reports primary MACE and components. These facts discriminate planned versus reported analyses; their multiplicity is not evidence of results-driven selection. They also cannot determine which components or early windows are eligible for the review's outcome domain without the review's independently specified eligibility criteria. The exact target fixes the selected result, not the complete eligible alternative set.

Both synthetic responses justified firm 5.2 No by “no alternative identified in the supplied dossier.” That statement does not guarantee that only one eligible measurement exists. This is a preservation-control design limitation and a shared scientific warrant weakness, not a new-guidance success or measured regression. A future control needs explicit independently defined eligibility, rather than equating absent dossier mention with uniqueness. The direct favorable-analysis control remains informative for 5.3, but was easy enough for both versions already to recognize.

## Remaining issues and readiness

Critical to scientific validation: affirmative chronology warrants must account for available counterevidence; independent outcome/result matching and eligibility are unresolved; raw registry/source gaps still limit some real cases; no comparative accuracy improvement has been established. The historical premise persistence report remains a reproduction item, but current public lifecycle tests give evidence against a universal loss mechanism.

Noncritical for this bounded checkpoint: the corrected stale-note comment, unchanged optional-null serialization, and the rejected old public singular counterpoint spelling. Historical revision/ignored-fixture and untouched lint/type failures remain separately documented; they do not establish this scientific behavior is correct.

Before a full benchmark, a small independent validation needs one or two new real cases with independently matched comparison/cohort/endpoint/time/population/analysis; verified raw Sources; a reviewer-defined eligible outcome/analysis set; and a prewritten source-supported warrant/counterwarrant for applicable plan and chronology. Include a credible affirmative plan inference and a credible uncertainty/late-choice contrast, preserving an actual or clearly synthetic positive result-driven-selection control. Freeze settings, cap paid usage, retain invalid drafts, compute labels with the server, and separately score source entailment and completeness. Passing two cases would remain qualification evidence, not an accuracy estimate. Full benchmark discussion and corpus alignment are still required.
