# D3 assumption comparison: implemented correction, rejected extra card

The shipped change replaces the existing SQ3.2 comparison example. Both sides now have post-hoc sensitivity analyses of the same approved Result and numerically close estimates. Only whether their changed/retained assumptions address the documented missingness mechanism differs. This removes an inappropriate prespecification requirement from D3 evidence and makes the scientific comparison more precise without adding a card, fields, validation gate, automatic answer or context stage. It neither requires an explicit MNAR analysis nor equates an unresolved assumption with proven bias.

A larger candidate added a separate SQ3.2 card with five unknown comparison slots. It was implemented and structurally tested, then rejected after the authorized matched diagnostic. Both outputs were frozen before adjudication. Both arms returned probably_yes for Attal and NefIgArd. The additional card did not produce an explicit comparison of changed and retained assumptions. The new response made Attal's conditional-MAR uncertainty more explicit, but omitted the control's alternative-assumption details and expressed uncertainty about coverage of all plausible mechanisms. That wording risks demanding more than Cochrane's context-dependent plausibility standard. There is no demonstrated accuracy improvement and insufficient benefit to justify the extra card. Its patch, exact model inputs, outputs, launch receipts and usage are preserved.

The final, smaller example correction was made after this diagnostic and was not retested with a paid response. It is retained for consistency with the official interpretation and the controlled scientific contrast, not for a claimed measured gain. No further diagnostic or full benchmark was run.

## Sources and interpretation

These are immutable October 1 cases from **/home/ali/Documents/Code/rob2-kit-benchmark**, not older main-repository evaluations. Attal's approved Result is the adjusted weekly pain-intensity effect over 24 weeks, not the point-specific week-24 contrast or the secondary responder outcome used by the meta-set label. This packet supports scientific diagnosis, not human-label agreement.

Attal's actual overall estimates are LOCF −0.768, two-point interpolation −0.723, three-point interpolation −0.752, likelihood mixed model −0.650 and MCMC MI −0.662. All reported intervals are below zero. This is real reassurance across different trajectory restrictions. The largest attenuation from LOCF is about 15.4%; that is descriptive, not a clinical or bias threshold. The original model received the numerical tables before saving its judgment, so table omission was not the demonstrated failure.

The report describes 131/1650 missing weekly records, more often late and in placebo. The CONSORT diagram records lack of efficacy among reasons for not receiving another administration or discontinuing. Those events do not establish which pain observations were missing. The week-24 safety assessment is not the primary-pain observation denominator. Two randomized placebo participants were excluded from the analyzed cohort, and the sensitivity tables do not demonstrate recovery of their outcomes. No participant-level missing-data reconstruction was invented.

The authors' failure to detect a baseline association does not rule out MNAR. Conversely, lack of an explicit MNAR departure does not invalidate conditional MAR if relevant observed information plausibly explains missingness. LOCF, interpolation and likelihood/MI do not literally share one identical assumption. Trial-specific imputation variables/configuration are insufficiently reported. The saved probably_yes warrant already acknowledged the absence of reported MNAR sensitivity; a scientifically certain reversal is not supported merely by that absence. Its mechanism-based reassurance remains incompletely explained.

NefIgArd is a legitimate contrasting control: the alternative analysis changes imputation for early discontinuers using treatment duration and later UPCR information. Its 5.14 estimate is close to the primary 5.05. This deserves mechanism-directed reassurance, with limitations judged in context rather than requiring every conceivable scenario.

The [official RoB2 guidance](https://www.riskofbias.info/welcome/rob-2-0-tool/current-version-of-rob-2), 22 August 2019, pp44–46, permits informative sensitivity evidence under plausible assumptions, including analyses by review authors. Prespecification is not an SQ3.2 prerequisite. The downloaded official PDF hash matches the production pack. [Cochrane Handbook §8.5](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-08) explains why mechanism plausibility depends on circumstances and observed information. [SAS MI documentation](https://support.sas.com/documentation/onlinedoc/stat/123/mi.pdf) supports the methodological discussion of conditional MAR, not a claim about the trial's unreported exact configuration.
