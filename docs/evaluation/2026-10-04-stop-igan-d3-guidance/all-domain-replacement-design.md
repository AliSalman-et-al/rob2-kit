# Conditional coherent scientific-pack replacement

The user authorizes replacing the original guidance across all five domains **if the authoritative approach works better**. STOP-IgAN does not meet that condition: prototype evidence interpretation regressed, and the control was incomplete. This is a reviewable design, not an implemented/default migration or authorization for further inference.

## Intended resulting architecture

Use one canonical, versioned scientific pack with complete official question elaborations and their provenance. Preserve question IDs, wording, allowed options, activation predicates, exact Result binding, source evidence, deterministic domain algorithms and the separately documented overall policy. Do not maintain two competing normative packs after adoption.

- Each question points to its complete official elaboration. Shared response guidance and domain scope/judgment explanations appear once per domain delivery, including their qualifications and exceptions.
- Track source version, PDF/content hash, physical/printed locators and full-source URL. Relevant FAQs are distinguished from the2019 guidance and carry their own capture provenance. Declare excerpt coverage accurately; a pagination-complete payload is not a claim that an entire source was reproduced.
- Remove maintainer answer anchors, decision rules, scientific thresholds, method requirements, competing comparison propositions/examples and duplicated skill guidance. Retain evidence navigation, source recovery, typed counts, source coordinates and the approved target/reported-result distinction.
- Skills describe tool operation, source inspection and submission. They point to the scientific pack instead of rephrasing its answer rules. Interface guidance must accurately reflect production activation: inactive answers can be retained and ignored; missing active answers prevent completion.
- Keep existing typed models and assemblers. Replace guidance fields where they are consumed, rather than adding a parallel evaluator, normative scoring layer or source-entailment heuristic. No keyword gate can prove a source supports an answer.

A canonical guidance change requires a new pack version/content hash that includes the delivered scientific guidance and FAQ provenance. Update protocol identity, generated assets, release verification and expected source metadata coherently. Saved assessments stay bound to their historical pack and retain original evidence/judgments; do not rewrite or retrospectively claim their scientific conclusions were improved. The current opt-in prototype's unchanged canonical hash is appropriate only as an explicitly separate delivery experiment, not as a final replacement identity.

## Evidence needed before adoption

First corroborate on a distinct, source-complete case such as already selected PIONEER-6, with the actual approved primary MACE Result and performed/conditional sensitivity distinction. Dossier completeness and exact raw hashes must precede inference. Agree on any paid-call scope separately; none is authorized by this document.

Evaluate method fidelity and source entailment independently of the desired label: observed versus analyzed/imputed, endpoint versus vital status, missingness assumptions versus method names, planned versus performed, possible versus likely dependence, reasonable probable judgments and retained uncertainty. Preserve legitimate control reassurance. Use production projection and unchanged algorithms to report labels, while separating path errors and unsupported premises. A tool-free diagnostic cannot substitute for native production behavior.

If corroboration supports improvement, transcribe and review official content for D1–D5, including D2 assignment/adherence applicability, D4 outcome-specific measurement influence, D5 multiplicity/selection and qualified overall confidence. Test preservation of options/dependencies and neutral D2/D5 behavior, plus targeted scientific examples with defensible disagreements. One same-label response, smaller context or passing structural tests is insufficient. Do not infer broad benchmark agreement from mismatched targets, force reference answers or launch the full benchmark without discussion.

If evidence remains null or worse, preserve the prototype experiment and fix demonstrated source/context problems without promoting a replacement or layering on case-specific normative repairs.
