25|with indomethacin or ibuprofen in the first 12 to

29|to increase survival without neurosensory impair-

31|approach is used, many infants will receive
32|treatment unnecessarily, since PDAs can close
33|spontaneously.8 Data on the treatment of infants
34|with a symptomatic PDA are limited, and reports
35|of improvements in clinical outcomes are lack-
36|ing.9 Consequently, the number of extremely pre-

38|cologically has decreased.10

40|diography,11 infants can now be screened to iden-

37|missing a significant number of events.

10|composite of CV Death, hospitalization for HF or an urgent HF visit.
11|The three components of the endpoint will be individually adjudicated by the CEA committee.
12|Patients who did not have an endpoint event will be censored at the earliest of date of WoC or
13|non-CV death when applicable, and otherwise at the earliest of date of last clinical event
14|assessment or PACD. Last clinical event assessment is defined as the last date when the event
15|assessment question for a potential heart failure event was completed on the eCRF event
16|assessment page. It is expected that patients alive and under study follow-up will have a
17|clinical event assessment at their SCV after PACD
18|In analysis of hospitalization for HF and urgent HF visit to examine the contribution of each
19|component of the composite endpoint, date of death from any cause will be an additional point
20|of censoring.
21|3.2
22|Secondary variables
23|The secondary endpoints are included in a hierarchical testing sequence following the primary
24|endpoint as ordered in Section 3.2.1 - 3.2.5.
25|3.2.1
26|The composite of CV death and hospitalization for HF
27|The efficacy variable is time from randomization to the first occurrence of any event in the
28|composite of CV death and hospitalization for HF. Patients who did not have the endpoint will
29|be censored by the same rule as for the primary endpoint.
30|3.2.2
31|Total number of (first and recurrent) hospitalizations for HF and CV death
32|The efficacy variable is the total number of first and recurrent hospitalizations for HF and CV
33|death, not including urgent HF visit.
34|For the analysis of recurrent heart failure hospitalization and CV death, the data will be
35|expressed in counting process style for input to the analysis as described in Section 4.2.4.1.
36|The time from randomization to end of follow-up/censoring will be split into one or more
37|interval with variables for start of interval, end of interval and a variable indicating if an event
38|occurred at the end of each respective interval, or if the patient was censored. Recurrent HF
39|hospitalizations, CV death and censoring processes all have continuous distributions so that
40|HF hospitalization and death cannot happen at the same time. If HF hospitalization and CV
41|death occurred at the same day, then only the CV death will be counted.
42|Patients who did not have the endpoint will be censored by the same rule as for the primary
43|endpoint.
