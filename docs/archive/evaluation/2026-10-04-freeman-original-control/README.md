## Exact warrant differences and sources

Control3.1: “This supports, but does not prove, that data were available for nearly all participants.” It explains: “Given the high reported treatment-visit completion and no evidence of extensive outcome loss, probably yes is reasonable, but exact arm-specific observed/imputed counts and the impact of missing values remain unknown.” Its retention basis is indirect support; imputation is attached as contradiction/counterevidence.

Prototype3.1: “Thus analyzed N cannot establish availability for all/nearly all; the actual observed/imputed outcome counts are unreported, so probable rather than definite no is appropriate.” It explicitly notes that all selected participants were analyzed and most completed treatment as counterevidence. Both acknowledge completion≠observation and imputed data count as missing. The prototype gives greater weight to uncertainty; the control gives greater weight to visit retention. Neither exact observation counts nor a formal effect bound is mandatory for a qualified response. These warrants permit reasonable scientific disagreement. The lack of exact counts does not automatically justifyPN orNI, and high completion does not provePY; the contextual inference is what must be reviewed.

Shared primary source p6lines39–48: “Across the trial, 77 (94%) of 82 participants completed treatment as evidenced by medication adherence at each treatment week … and attending all treatment week visits within 2 days of the scheduled appointment.” This is whole-trial retention across all doses, not an exact selected-arm observation fraction. Figure1/mainp5 independently gives selected completion21/23 placebo and23/24CBD400. Thus the control's indirect reassurance is informative but its pooled77/82 figure should not become a selected47participant endpoint count. Neither run made that conversion in typed data.

Shared primary source p4lines108–114: “Missing data were handled using Bayesian multiple imputation under the assumption of missing at random. The missing outcomes were automatically simulated from the Bayesian procedure, in accordance with the modelling and distributional assumptions.” This establishes a missing-data method, not the imputed number or automatic unbiasedness. The original control closes at3.1PY, so no3.2 response is required. It cannot be credited with superior3.2 interpretation merely for reading more methods.

Prototype3.2: “This is a model for missing values, not evidence that the result is unbiased under plausible departures from MAR; the described sensitivity analyses add age and sex, not assumptions about missingness.” Its PN is defensible given mechanism uncertainty, but should weigh the reported model components rather than implying a mandatory namedMNAR sensitivity. Author manuscript p7lines19–25 states: “All analyses included time as a fixed effect (treatment weeks1–4) and were adjusted for baseline scores at week0. Participant was fitted as a random intercept.” It then describes the gamma model and posthocage/sex adjustment. ## Conclusion and different architecture hypothesis

The paired qualitative evidence is mixed and ambiguous: no credible improvement warrant is established, no matched human-label target comparison was made, and the condition for expanding the prototype across all domains is not met. Keep the prototype opt-in; keep useful typed completion/visual provenance representation without attributing this contrast to it.

No source/Result binding loss or unknown→zero defect was found. Both exact Result identities, source pixels, visual provenance, row counts and active gates survive. A case-specific parser, preferred label, forcedNI, mandatory exhaustive reading or another prose patch would not address the observed reasoning problems.

A concrete next architecture hypothesis is to extend the existing `WorkingPremiseRecord.observations`/`WorkingNote` path with typed Result/group/stage/window scope and reported-versus-inferred meaning, then link those observations to answer warrants. Record whole-trial retention as context, selected-arm reasons separately, and actual imputation-model components as source facts before clinical inference. Group IDs can be checked against approved groups; source coordinates remain auditable, and such checks must never be advertised as automatic PDF entailment. This reuses existing working state rather than adding a parallel agent/pipeline, permits unknown counts and contextual probable judgments, and targets factual scope/method omissions directly. A neutral test should check whether facts from an excluded arm stay contextual and whether known model components survive into the relevant inference. This hypothesis remains unimplemented; no further paid call was made in this comparison.
