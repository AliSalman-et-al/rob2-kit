# D2/D3/D5 source-authority audit

Compared the complete applicable official background and elaborations with current
delivered source cards and packaged references: assignment-effect D2, pp.21–29;
D3, pp.39–46; D5, pp.58–65. Settled D2.3/D2.6/D2.7/D3.2 and generic D5 chronology
issues were excluded. The source receipt records the no-change decisions for D2/D3.
No official-D3 prototype superiority claim or rollout is made.

A concrete D5 contradiction was replaced at its origin: cards required independently
eligible alternatives yet froze the selected Result's time window and analysis scope.
Cochrane sections8.1–8.1.2 (pp.58–60) and Box11 (pp.63–65) explicitly permit different
eligible scales, time points, final/change values, ANCOVA and adjustment strategies.
The selected Result stays the assessment target; it cannot retrospectively erase its
eligible alternatives. Conversely, independently prespecified review criteria or a
hierarchy can exclude an alternative, so preference for a reported eligible result
over that ineligible option does not itself bias the result. Unknown eligibility
remains unknown. Primary/secondary labels or the selected analysis do not establish
independent criteria. No new fields, gates or forced answers are introduced.

The same official section distinguishes selection within an outcome domain from
non-reporting of another domain. An unreported anxiety domain does not by itself put
a reported depression estimate at risk; eligible depression scales and analyses still
require appraisal. This missing boundary is explicit in source5.2 and selection.md.

Two bounded first native D5 submissions from the actual Code October1 corpus
corroborate the relevance. Bagg compares18/26/52-week measurements; Attal compares
several reported missing-data analyses. Freezing these dimensions would remove
alternatives their reasoning considers. Attal's primary/secondary distinction
illustrates an eligibility warrant needing independent support, rather than a
demonstrated wrong label. Full submissions, original log paths/line numbers and SHA
hashes are retained. Original data are untouched. These illustrate interpretation;
they are neither benchmark targets nor behavioral validation of the correction.
