13: 6.13
14: Trial Termination Visit
15: Investigators will be informed by the DCRI and DTU Coordinating Centers as to when the Trial 
16: Termination Visit is to be completed, and will schedule all patients for the Trial Termination 
17: Visit.  Based on the primary endpoint event cut-off date, a window of time for conduct of the 
18: Trial Termination Visits will be established; this window will be a period of time following the 
19: primary endpoint event cut-off date.  All patients will be expected to have follow-up until this 
20: visit.  At the Trial Termination Visit, all patients must be discontinued from trial medication; the 
21: investigator should ensure that the patient receives appropriate standard of care. For procedural 
22: details of the Trial Termination Visit, refer to Appendix 2.
23: NOTE:  All patients should have a Trial Termination Visit. Patients who have discontinued 
24: trial medication prior to the end of the trial must, at minimum, have a Trial Termination
25: telephone contact.
26: If a patient fails to return or otherwise becomes difficult to contact, it is the investigator’s 
27: responsibility to make every effort to maintain contact so that at the end of the trial the patient 
28: can be located to determine status and to obtain necessary information for serious adverse 
29: experience reporting and/or endpoint adjudication as of the Trial Termination Visit.
30: 6.14
31: Post-Treatment Telephone Contact
32: After the Trial Termination Visit, patients will be contacted by telephone to check for any 
33: Clinical Events, serious adverse experiences and hospitalizations that occurred within 70 days 
34: after the administration of the last dose of trial medication. 
35: For patients who have been 
36: discontinued from trial medication for more than 70 days as of the Trial Termination Visit, the 
37: Trial Termination Visit will be the final study follow-up.  Clinical Events (as defined in Protocol 
38: Appendix 1) will continue to be collected and reported in the same way as they have been 
39: throughout the entire study (i.e., prior to the cut-off date) (see Section 10.3.1).
40: 6.15
41: Withdrawal of Consent
42: Patients who request to discontinue study treatment will remain in the study and must continue to 
43: be followed for protocol specified follow-up procedures. The only exception to this is when a 
44: patient explicitly withdraws consent for any further contact with him/her or persons previously 
45: authorized by patient to provide this information. 
46: Patients should notify the investigator of the decision to withdraw consent from future follow-up 
47: in writing, whenever possible. The withdrawal of consent should be explained in detail in the 
48: medical records by the investigator and entered on the appropriate eCRF page. In the event that 
49: vital status (whether the patient is alive or dead) is being measured, publicly available 
50: information should be used to determine vital status only as appropriately directed in accordance 
51: with local law.
