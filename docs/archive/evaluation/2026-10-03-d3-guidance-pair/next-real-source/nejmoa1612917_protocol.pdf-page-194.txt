22: Planned Subgroup Analyses
23: Subgroup analyses for the primary CV composite endpoint will be performed on the ITT 
24: population in order to explore whether the treatment effect on the risk of developing CV 
25: events is consistent across subgroups.
26: Subgroup analyses to evaluate variation in treatment effect will be performed on the basis of 
27: tests for interaction using the Cox proportional hazards model stratified by prior CV event at 
28: randomization based on IVRS (not applicable for the first subgroup listed) with terms for 
29: treatment group, the subgroup variable and treatment by subgroup variable interaction. P-
30: values for the interaction with treatment for each of these subgroup variables will be provided 
31: and p-value of <0.1 will be considered as significant interaction. However, it is recognized 
32: that testing many subgroups can yield spurious false positive outcomes so that any significant 
33: interaction will be further examined to better understand its nature. If some subgroups have 
34: very few patients with events, they may be excluded from the interaction test.
35: Additionally, treatment effects within each subgroup will be examined separately using Cox 
36: proportional hazards models stratified by prior CV event at randomization based on IVRS (not 
37: applicable for the first subgroup listed) with terms for treatment group. Event rates by 
38: treatment and HRs with 95% confidence intervals will be reported for each subgroup. Forest 
39: plots will be generated displaying the estimated hazard ratios and 95% confidence intervals 
40: for each subgroup will be presented.
