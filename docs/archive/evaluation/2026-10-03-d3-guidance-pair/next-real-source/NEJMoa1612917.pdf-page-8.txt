44: Safety Outcomes
45: No clinically relevant between-group differences 
46: were seen in the incidence of serious adverse 
47: events or events of clinical interest (Table 2, and 
48: Table S7 in the Supplementary Appendix). Con-
49: firmed events of acute pancreatitis were uncom-
50: mon, and the number of patients who had con-
51: firmed acute pancreatitis was similar in the 
52: exenatide group and the placebo group (26 and 22, 
53: respectively), as were the number of patients 
54: who had cancers overall (355 and 361) and the 
55: numbers of patients who had pancreatic cancer 
56: (15 and 16) and medullary thyroid carcinoma 
57: (2 and 1). More patients in the exenatide group 
58: than in the placebo group had thyroid papillary 
59: carcinomas (10 vs. 4).
60: The rate of severe hypoglycemia did not differ 
61: significantly between the two groups, either 
62: when measured as the first event only (1.0 events 
63: per 100 patient-years in the exenatide group and 
64: 0.9 events per 100 patient-years in the placebo 
65: group) or when recurrent events were included 
66: (1.6 events per 100 patient-years and 1.8 events 
67: per 100 patient-years, respectively; risk ratio, 
68: 0.85; 95% CI, 0.67 to 1.08) (Table 2).
69: Discussion
70: In this pragmatic, multinational, cardiovascular 
71: outcomes trial, which was performed in a usual-
72: care setting among patients with type 2 diabetes, 
73: with or without previous cardiovascular disease, 
74: the addition of once-weekly extended-release 
75: exenatide to usual care was compared with usual 
76: care alone for the management of diabetes and 
77: cardiovascular risk factors over a median of 
78: 3.2 years of follow-up; the results showed that 
79: exenatide was noninferior to placebo with respect 
80: to cardiovascular safety but was not superior to 
81: placebo with respect to efficacy. The risk of death 
82: from any cause was 6.9% in the exenatide group 
83: and 7.9% in the placebo group (hazard ratio, 0.86; 
84: 95% CI, 0.77 to 0.97); this difference was not 
85: considered to be statistically significant on the 
86: basis of the hierarchical testing plan. Events of 
87: acute pancreatitis, pancreatic cancer, and medul-
88: lary thyroid carcinoma were uncommon, with 
89: similar rates in the two groups.
