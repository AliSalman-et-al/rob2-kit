# Composite measurement guidance fidelity

Direct inspection of the authoritative 22 August 2019 Cochrane PDF, section 7.1,
printed p52, confirmed that composite appraisal considers component frequency or
contribution and risk from the most influential components. The complete delivered
D4 cards and packaged measurement reference preserved component concerns but omitted
that weighting qualification; the reference's categorical non-override wording
could overemphasize a minor subjective component.

The source cards for method suitability, possible influence and likely influence,
and the delivered reference, now require component-specific pathways followed by
relative frequency/contribution and most-influential-component reasoning. Unknown
contributions remain unknown. Assessor awareness, susceptibility, possibility and
likelihood remain separate. No subjective component automatically makes a composite
High. A neutral paired premise contrasts objective versus susceptible predominance
without assigning answers or labels to either, or naming a benchmark trial.

Operational guidance version is 1.0.12. Both verifiers retain the exact former
pack descriptors and pin the new current hash; no artifacts or judgments were
rewritten. Authoritative PDF SHA256:
`a9e9c4fdc4be2d29b5c0a1a6b828e09f2014a34f6d5c302a532f6153ea0fd670`.
