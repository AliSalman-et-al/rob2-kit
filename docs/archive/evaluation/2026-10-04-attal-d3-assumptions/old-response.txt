**SQ3.2 — evidence that the result was not biased by missing outcome data**

- **attal-2016: probably_yes.** The article reports that primary-outcome analyses used LOCF, which alone does not establish absence of bias. But a post-hoc sensitivity analysis compared LOCF with linear interpolation, maximum-likelihood analysis and MCMC multiple imputation; all yielded similar treatment effects (supplement, physical pp. 13–14, lines 4–10, 19–24, 47–52, 103–108; p. 14, lines 3–8). This supports robustness, though the report does not establish that these analyses span plausible assumptions matched to the documented missingness. Missing diary records were 7.9%, more common late and in placebo; the authors classified the pattern as MAR and reported no detected baseline effect on missingness (supplement, p. 15, lines 9–15). The LOCF analysis may overestimate efficacy (article, p. 10, lines 26–33). **Material unknown:** the sensitivity analysis’s assumptions and their adequacy for the stated missingness pattern are not sufficiently detailed. **Answer: probably_yes.**

- **nefigard: probably_yes.** The primary result used multiple imputation, and prespecified sensitivity analyses varied missing-data assumptions. An alternative-assumptions analysis produced a similar estimate (5.14 versus 5.05); MMRM also gave a similar result (5.16) (supplement, physical p. 10, lines 19–24, 37–50, 77–86). The supplement says sensitivity and supplementary analyses were consistent with the primary result (p. 7, lines 9–14). These analyses support robustness, but the evidence does not fully establish that assumptions cover plausible missing-outcome mechanisms: the described alternative assumptions focus on early treatment discontinuation, while the primary imputation conditions on previous eGFR measurements (article, physical p. 4, lines 86–95; supplement, p. 10, lines 82–86). **Material unknown:** the supplied excerpts do not fully characterize the missingness mechanisms or the complete range of plausible assumptions tested. **Answer: probably_yes.**
