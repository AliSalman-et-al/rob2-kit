### Attal 2016 — **probably_yes**
**Coordinates:** Main article, physical p. 4, lines 40–63 and p. 10, lines 18–33; supplement, physical pp. 13–14, lines 4–10, 19–30 and 3–33; supplement p. 15, lines 7–15.

The post-hoc sensitivity analysis reports similar primary-result estimates across LOCF, interpolation, maximum-likelihood/mixed-model, and MCMC multiple-imputation methods (treatment estimates about −0.65 to −0.77, all statistically significant). The supplement reports 131/1,650 weekly pain records missing, mostly late in follow-up and more often in placebo; authors characterized the pattern as MAR, with no detected baseline effect. This provides some reassurance for the approved 24-week result, beyond LOCF alone.

**Material unknowns:** The sensitivity analyses’ assumptions are not fully specified, and the report does not establish whether they adequately address missingness related to unobserved pain values. The missingness mechanism characterization does not itself establish that MAR is plausible conditional on observed information.

### NefIgArd — **probably_yes**
**Coordinates:** Main article, physical p. 4, lines 75–104 and p. 7, lines 73–77; supplement, physical pp. 4–5, lines 60–71 and 1–24; p. 7, lines 5–14; p. 10, lines 19–26 and 45–53, 77–86.

The primary eGFR analysis multiply imputed missing values conditional on previous eGFR measurements. Prespecified sensitivity analyses varied missing-data assumptions; the alternative-assumptions analysis reported a 5.14 treatment difference (95% CI 3.24–7.58), close to the primary 5.05 (3.24–7.38). Other sensitivity results were also reported as consistent. This supports reassurance for the approved two-year eGFR result.

**Material unknowns:** The supplied pages do not fully describe the primary imputation assumptions or all assumptions changed and retained in sensitivity analyses. In particular, the alternative assumptions described for early treatment discontinuation do not establish how all plausible missing-outcome mechanisms were covered.
