## Proposal Review

**Trial:** AVATAR (Banovic et al., *Circulation* 2022)  
**Selected Result:** Exact primary intention-to-treat result

The article defines the primary endpoint as a composite of all-cause death, acute myocardial infarction, stroke, or unplanned heart-failure hospitalization requiring intravenous diuretics or inotropes. It describes the analysis as time to first MACE and reports the primary hazard ratio for early surgical aortic valve replacement versus conservative management:

- **Population:** all 157 randomized patients—78 assigned to early surgery and 79 to conservative treatment
- **Follow-up:** data collection ended May 2021
- **Hazard ratio:** 0.46
- **95% CI:** 0.23–0.90

The article reports all-cause death and heart-failure hospitalization separately as secondary time-to-event outcomes; neither is the selected Result. The source’s definition, analysis description, and primary-outcome table support the selected composite. The saved Proposal classifies it as exact.
