## Three demonstrated failures and next direction

1. **Unsupported promotion of evidence into a stronger premise:** RECOVERY 5.1 treats early planning as sufficient despite an unresolved selected-model discrepancy; Bendix 2.6 treats contactability as proof of measurement omission. Current contracts preserve citations/unknowns but do not adjudicate entailment. This remains model judgment.
2. **D3 effect importance:** DELIVER's explicit instruction still yields small-fraction/near-balance reasoning without a source-supported event-risk or effect-scale warrant. Denominator separation works; the scientific inference does not yet meet the criterion.
3. **Material evidence integration at closure:** EXSCEL's later SAP sensitivity evidence does not prompt revisiting D3. EMPEROR stops before the definition's cross-page alternatives yet asserts clarity. HOST-EXAM demonstrates a milder omission: known conditional imputation is omitted from an otherwise defensible NI warrant.

Recommend one coherent next implementation direction: simplify source-to-warrant context and closure review using existing evidence records. Reuse unchanged source passages, focus context on active questions, and colocate planned/executed/reported facts, their trigger and unknowns beside the exact claim. At closure surface material contradictions or new facts against already saved domain warrants. Avoid automatic method matching, forced appendix passes, new scientific gates or more repeated premise surfaces. First replay the retained EXSCEL/HOST-EXAM/EMPEROR traces offline; assess entailment and materiality independently, and measure calls/context saved. Only then discuss one matched, frozen, preflight-checked diagnostic. More prose/premise additions and repeated same-case paid repairs currently have low demonstrated marginal return.
