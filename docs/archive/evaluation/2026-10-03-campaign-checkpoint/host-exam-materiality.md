## Exact saved warrants

New 5.1:

> The supplement's statistical-analysis section prespecifies the 2-year primary outcome and ITT/per-protocol populations and describes Kaplan–Meier/log-rank analysis; the report describes the primary result using Cox and Kaplan–Meier methods. But the captured plan has no located finalization date, and the report/supplement do not establish when investigators first had unblinded outcome data. Approval and a study period are not evidence of SAP finalization or investigator access. Correspondence and the required chronology therefore remain uncertain; neither definitive nor probable timing is supportable.

Archived 5.1:

> A protocol/SAP is available and specifies a 24-month endpoint analysis in ITT and per-protocol populations. However, participants and investigators were unmasked, and the captured protocol/SAP has no date or amendment history establishing that the final analysis plan preceded unblinded outcome access. Registry submission and study-start dates do not date this SAP version.

New 5.3:

> The plan calls for ITT and per-protocol analysis, and the report says primary/secondary endpoints were analyzed in both populations and reports similar per-protocol results. The report additionally describes Cox and Kaplan–Meier methods, while the inspected SAP passage specifies Kaplan–Meier/log-rank; it does not clearly establish the full eligible set of models or why the HR model was used. There is no inspected evidence that a result-favorable subset was chosen, but the plan/report detail is insufficient to conclude all eligible analyses were specified and reported. Thus neither result-driven selection nor its absence is supportable.

Archived 5.3:

> The primary ITT analysis and supportive per-protocol result are both reported, with similar effects, and the article reports both a Cox hazard ratio and log-rank result. This does not show result-based selection. However, the protocol also planned an imputation sensitivity analysis that is not located in the report, and the reported Cox estimate is not specified in the captured SAP. The available intentions and reporting therefore do not fully resolve the eligible analysis set.

## Adjudication

The omitted imputation passage is material to completeness of the eligible-analysis account in 5.3. The archived warrant explicitly preserves a known planned sensitivity analysis and its unresolved reporting; the new warrant replaces that specificity with a broader uncertainty statement. Neither new warrant falsely says no sensitivity analysis was planned. NI remains defensible on the saved evidence; omission alone does not compel a different label or prove selective reporting. The earlier run report's failed completeness criterion should therefore be read as an underexplained warrant, not a demonstrated false-absence assertion.

For 5.1, the omission does not invalidate the specific observation that the selected Cox HR is not explicitly specified in the captured KM/log-rank plan. The imputation sensitivity analysis is a different planned analysis, not evidence that the primary Cox model corresponded to a finalized pre-access plan. Unknowns remain SAP version/finalization/amendments relative to outcome access and whether Cox was covered by a legitimate amendment. Unmasking or absent exact timestamps alone cannot establish late planning or require NI universally.

For 5.3, retain the known imputation plan and investigate execution/reporting, the eligible Cox model and reasons for any changes. Do not promote planned imputation into performed robustness, or missing reporting into result-driven selection. Delivery was complete; this finding calls for better evidence integration, not a retrieval repair.
