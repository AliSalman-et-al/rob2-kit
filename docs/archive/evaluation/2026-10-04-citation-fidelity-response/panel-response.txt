The focused claim is directly supported by the process-outcomes table: tests were not performed for 13/86 eligible infants in the ibuprofen group and 13/94 in placebo (sh_ec3fcba1a06e0ddc, p. 7, lines 85–94). Similar proportions describes these figures; it does not establish why tests were missed or that every participant had the same assessment opportunity. The cited passages do not report group-specific test methods or thresholds; that is absence of detail, not evidence of different methods. Blinding is not established by these cited passages. The broader statement that outcome data were missing for some participants is supported: the table footnote reports missing oxygen-requirement information for five ibuprofen and three placebo infants (sh_ec3fcba1a06e0ddc, p. 6, lines 105–106). The smallest supported wording is the focused claim with its counts and denominators; avoid inferring equivalent measurement from similar proportions alone.
