AURORA is a public synthetic, individually randomised, parallel-group Trial of intervention A versus usual care.

Participants were assigned by a computer-generated sequence using variable blocks. The independent service released the next allocation after eligibility was recorded. The primary result is all-cause mortality at 30 days in the intention-to-treat population: 18/240 versus 24/238.

Participants and treating clinicians could see the assigned intervention. The outcome assessor used the prespecified vital-status definition and was unaware of allocation. Five participants withdrew before day 30; the report states that vital status was obtained for all five through the registry.

The protocol specified mortality at 30 days as the primary result. The report also gives an exploratory 12-month estimate. The 30-day estimate is the result-bearing analysis for this dossier.
