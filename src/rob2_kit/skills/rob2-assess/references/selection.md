# Assess selection of the reported result

Use this reference for Domain 5.

Compare the approved Result with a prespecified analysis plan that existed before unblinded outcome data were available. Assess selection among eligible measurements, time points, definitions, analyses, and effect estimates. A reported endpoint definition, time origin, analysis population, or result estimate does not prove that this choice was prespecified or that no alternative analyses existed. A planned method does not prove that investigators followed it. Keep each proposition separate and cite the exact selected fragment that proves it.

The active question card is authoritative. Its official excerpt and locator
define the proposition; the seeds and pitfalls below guide retrieval.

The approved Result fixes the target for this Domain. Do not switch endpoints to match an easier source passage.

For question 5.1, compare the approved Result with the exact planned
measurement, time point, population, and analysis passages. A single detailed
plan passage can be sufficient. If the plan is split across a dated protocol
and SAP, cite both independent passage references and explain what each
establishes. A registration identifier, endpoint label, or statement that an
analysis was prespecified does not by itself establish the plan, its timing
before unblinding, or correspondence to the reported result.

For a composite, component, or related endpoint, compare the exact definition,
assessment schedule, analysis population, censoring rules, and time point. A
non-exact relation still requires the researcher's confirmed Result card.

Claims about protocol or SAP prespecification require the exact selected passage from the dated protocol or SAP, with its document scope and date clear. A results paper saying that an analysis was prespecified is not the protocol/SAP passage itself unless it is the only source and the claim is limited to what that paper reports. Absence of a protocol or analysis plan is an information limit. It does not prove selective reporting. Record the exact search account and the limitation.

## Optional search leads from official guidance

- **Official guidance, p. 63, Box 11, question 5.1:** Consider timing and plan
  correspondence terms such as `pre-specified analysis plan`, `analysis intentions`,
  `finalized`, `unblinded outcome data`, `before`, and `unrelated to results`.
- **Official guidance, pp. 63-64, Box 11, question 5.2:** Consider measurement
  multiplicity terms such as `multiple eligible outcome measurements`, `scales`,
  `definitions`, `time points`, `subset`, `justification`, and `selected on the
  basis of the results`.
- **Official guidance, pp. 64-65, Box 11, question 5.3:** Consider analysis
  multiplicity terms such as `multiple eligible analyses`, `adjustment`, `transformation`,
  `composite definitions`, `missing-data strategies`, `subset`, and `selected
  on the basis of the results`.

When useful, group prespecification, eligible measurements, and eligible
analyses terms separately. Refine a truncated discovery search before concluding
that no plan, measurement, or analysis is reported.

## Pitfalls

The approved Result fixes the target. Do not switch endpoints to match an easier
passage. An endpoint definition, time origin, analysis population, or result
estimate does not prove prespecification or rule out alternatives. A protocol or
SAP label without its dated detail does not establish the plan. Keep multiple
eligible measurements separate from multiple eligible analyses.
