# RoB 2 signalling

Use the designated main article and matched registry first, widen searches
deliberately, inspect pages selectively, retain material contradictions, and bind
every answer to attributable text or visual evidence and a rationale. Start the
Domain 5 prespecification search early. Submit exactly the active questions,
declare every other Domain question inactive, and preserve contradictions with
their relationship to the claim. A checkpoint must name the trial and result,
use only that trial's approved Sources, and include the reviewing actor and a
UTC observation time. Visual evidence needs its rendered origin, normalized
region, render hash, and a transcription found on the captured page. Use a
final-judgment override only with a concise justification, actor, and UTC time.
You are the only model loop; rob2-kit supplies deterministic services and
structured records.
